## Overall

The draft is a stronger *voice sample* than the current template pages. Several destination paragraphs would be usable after fact-stripping. It is not a publishable replacement pack.

It fails the original brief on the four things that matter for this site:

1. It invents or inflates operational facts the owner has not confirmed.
2. It overpromises safety — including on the homepage, About, and Panjshir.
3. It leans on the generic travel register the brief banned (`unforgettable`, `ultimate … adventure`, `the tour that changes everything`).
4. It only covers a slice of the site. CSV leaks, `$Inquire`, Safety, food boilerplate, specialist tours, and broken destination–tour links are untouched.

Tone target from the original brief: knowledgeable, grounded, credible, reassuring without guarantees, premium without luxury theater, adventurous without sensationalizing. This draft is closer to a brochure for a country that is not Afghanistan.

Keep the good lines. Kill the guarantees. Map every tour name to a live slug. Mark every unconfirmed inclusion `[OWNER INPUT REQUIRED]`.

## Critical fixes (show-stoppers)

### 1. Safety language that cannot go live

Original rule: no security guarantees; prefer planning, conditions change, informed decisions.

| Draft line | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| “Afghanistan’s most trusted licensed tour operator” | Unprovable superlative | “A licensed Kabul-based tour operator” |
| “secure, meaningful journeys” | Security promise | “planned journeys” / “carefully routed journeys” |
| “Safety-First Operations” + “vetted in real time” + “so you don’t have to” | Implies the traveler can stop paying attention | “Routes are reviewed before departure and adjusted when road, weather, or access conditions change.” |
| “We will keep you safe.” | Direct guarantee | Delete. Or: “We plan for conditions. We do not sell certainty.” |
| Panjshir “Security: Historically safe” | False frame after 2021; legally ugly | “Access is assessed before each departure.” |
| Herat “Security: Vetted daily” | Procedure you have not confirmed for publication | Omit, or “routing reviewed before travel” |
| Kabul “Security: Real-time monitoring” | Ops claim as marketing | Omit from public table |
| Trust bar “Visa & LOI Support Included” | Live visa page: you do not issue visas. LOI-on-every-tour is still unanswered | “Letter of Invitation support where applicable. We do not issue visas.” |
| “Your safety is planned, not promised.” | Good. Keep. | Keep. |

Do not make safety the brand. The original brief said so explicitly.

### 4. Fact errors inside the pretty sentences

| Claim | Issue |
|---|---|

| Live Day 2 lists Salang on the Bamyan drive | Salang is the *north* road. Kabul–Bamyan is Wardak / Hajigak. Draft is geographically better than the live page; still don’t invent elevation. |
