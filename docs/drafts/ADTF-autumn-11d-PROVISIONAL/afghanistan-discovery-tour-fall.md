### Afghanistan Discovery Tour Autumn (provisional route)

Eleven autumn days by road: Kabul, the Ghaznavid capital at Ghazni, Kandahar in pomegranate season, then the Bamyan valley and Band-e-Amir.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 11 days / 10 nights
- Route: Kabul → Ghazni → Kandahar → Kabul → Bamyan → Band-e-Amir → Kabul
- Transport: private 4WD throughout; no domestic flights
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Sep-Nov

### Where You Stay

- Kabul: nights 1, 2, 6 and 10
- Ghazni: night 3
- Kandahar: nights 4 and 5
- Bamyan: nights 7, 8 and 9

### What This Tour Is For

Autumn harvests, heritage and regional culture: the minarets and tombs of Ghazni, Kandahar’s Durrani monuments and pomegranate orchards, and the Bamyan valley before winter.

### Planning Notes

Days 3, 4 and 6 are long road days. Overnight availability in Ghazni and Kandahar and the feasibility of each road day are confirmed before any departure is offered. Band-e-Amir depends on access. The Shrine of the Cloak is seen from outside where permitted, and the pomegranate harvest experience is seasonal.
