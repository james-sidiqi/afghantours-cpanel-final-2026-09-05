# Bala Hisar of Balkh hero (replaced 22 Nov 2026)

`ancient-city-balkh-walls.webp` was the previous public hero and card for `/attractions/balkh-bala-hisar-ancient-walls/` (a walk along the top of the earthen wall). It had also been used in the Ancient City of Balkh experience gallery after that experience hero was changed to James’s city overview aerial.

James supplied a replacement aerial on 22 Nov 2026 (t467u), looking along the wall from the octagonal pavilion. The published attraction page now uses `owner-20261122-wall-pavilion-aerial.webp` as the hero and card. This file is kept here for recovery and is not a public site asset.
