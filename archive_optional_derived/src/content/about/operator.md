## Operational Approach

Our operations are coordinated directly from Afghanistan through local networks, regional contacts, guides, drivers, and hospitality partners familiar with the realities of travel throughout the country.

This local operational presence allows us to plan routes realistically, adapt to changing conditions when necessary, and provide travelers with direct support before and during their journey.
