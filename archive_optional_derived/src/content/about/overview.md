## About AfghanTours

AfghanTours.com is a Kabul-based licensed Afghan tour operator focused on professionally coordinated travel across Afghanistan.

Built through a close collaboration between Afghan and international team members, AfghanTours was created to help travelers experience Afghanistan in a realistic, respectful, and well-supported way — beyond headlines and assumptions.

Our journeys are designed around local knowledge, operational planning, cultural understanding, and flexibility. From the historic cities of Herat and Balkh to the mountain valleys of Bamyan, Panjshir, and Nuristan, we help travelers access parts of Afghanistan that remain largely unseen by the outside world.

We work with experienced local guides, drivers, regional coordinators, hotels, and community contacts across multiple provinces to support a wide range of travel styles, including:

- Cultural and historical journeys  
- Photography and documentary travel  
- Backcountry and mountain expeditions  
- Research and media support trips  
- Custom private itineraries  

AfghanTours emphasizes realistic planning, transparent communication, and responsible travel practices. Routes, accommodations, permits, seasonal access, and regional conditions are continuously reviewed throughout the planning process.

Our goal is simple: to help respectful travelers experience the depth, hospitality, landscapes, and history of Afghanistan through carefully coordinated travel grounded in local experience.
