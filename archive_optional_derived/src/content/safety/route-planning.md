## Purpose

All travel within Afghanistan requires active route planning and continuous operational assessment. Conditions can vary significantly depending on weather, season, road quality, local activity, infrastructure limitations, and regional coordination requirements.

Routes are planned using current operational information, local contacts, road condition assessments, seasonal accessibility, and daylight movement considerations. Travel timing may change depending on snowfall, flooding, construction, traffic congestion, checkpoints, or temporary local restrictions.

Whenever possible, long-distance overland movement is conducted during daylight hours to improve visibility, reduce operational risk, and allow greater flexibility for route adjustments if required. In mountainous regions such as Bamyan, the central highlands, Salang Pass, Nuristan, or remote provincial roads, weather and road conditions may shift rapidly.

Alternative routing options may occasionally be required to maintain operational flexibility and traveler comfort. In some situations, movement schedules, overnight locations, or activity sequencing may be adjusted based on evolving conditions or local coordination recommendations.

Travelers should expect expedition-style flexibility rather than rigid fixed-route tourism operations.
