## Overview
Mountain wilderness and remnants of a medieval empire.

---

## Why Visit
Ghor is a remote and mountainous province known for the Minaret of Jam and the legacy of the Ghorid dynasty. Its rugged terrain and sparse settlements preserve a sense of isolation and historical intrigue.

---

## Key Attractions
- Minaret of Jam
- Chehel Burj
- Rural Ghor Highlands
