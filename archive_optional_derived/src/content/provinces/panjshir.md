## Overview
Iconic valley, emerald rivers, and mountain scenery.

---

## Why Visit
Panjshir is one of Afghanistan’s most visually striking valleys, known for steep mountain walls, flowing rivers, and historic resistance narratives. Its natural beauty makes it a popular day-trip destination from Kabul.
