## Overview
Remote mountain landscapes, Wakhan culture, and high-altitude Silk Road routes.

---

## Why Visit
Badakhshan is Afghanistan’s northeastern frontier, defined by towering Pamir and Hindu Kush peaks, ancient trade routes, and the culturally distinct Wakhan Corridor. The province offers dramatic scenery, alpine valleys, and a glimpse into some of the country’s most remote and preserved traditions.

- Badakhshan Mountains & Lakes
- The Wakhan Corridor
