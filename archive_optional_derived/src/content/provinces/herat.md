## Overview
Timurid architecture, Persian influence, and cultural refinement.

---

## Why Visit
Herat is Afghanistan’s western cultural capital, celebrated for its Timurid monuments, historic old city, and artistic traditions. With strong historical ties to Persia and Central Asia, Herat offers some of the country’s most refined urban heritage.

---

## Key Attractions
- Herat Old City & Bazaars
- Herat Citadel
- Herat Friday Mosque
- Herat Minarets
