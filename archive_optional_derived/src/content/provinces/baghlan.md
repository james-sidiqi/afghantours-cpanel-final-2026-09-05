## Overview
Green valleys, ancient sites, and a crossroads of northern Afghanistan.

---

## Why Visit
Baghlan connects the central highlands with the northern provinces and has long served as a strategic and cultural crossroads. Fertile valleys, historical remains, and proximity to Silk Road corridors give the province both natural beauty and historical depth.
