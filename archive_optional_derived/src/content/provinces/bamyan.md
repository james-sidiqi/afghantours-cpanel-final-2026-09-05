## Overview
Highland landscapes, UNESCO heritage, and Afghanistan’s premier national park.

---

## Why Visit
Bamyan lies in the central highlands and is renowned for its dramatic valleys, archaeological heritage, and access to Band-e-Amir National Park. Once a major Buddhist center on the Silk Road, Bamyan today combines history, nature, and a slower pace of life.

---

## Key Attractions
- Buddha Niches of Bamyan
- Shahr-e-Gholghola
- Shahr-e-Zohak
- Band-e-Amir National Park
- Koh-e-Baba Mountain Range
- Dragon Valley
