## Overview
Forested mountains and unique cultural heritage.

---

## Why Visit
Nuristan is one of Afghanistan’s most culturally distinct provinces, set amid forested mountains and remote valleys. Its traditions, architecture, and landscapes differ markedly from the rest of the country.

---

## Key Attractions
- Parun Valley
- Bashgal Valley
- Barg-e Matal & Lakes
- Landai Sin & Mandol Lakes
