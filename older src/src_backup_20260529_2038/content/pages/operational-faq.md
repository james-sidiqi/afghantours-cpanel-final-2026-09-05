### Operational FAQ

### Is travel in Afghanistan fixed or flexible?

Travel in Afghanistan requires operational flexibility and realistic expectations.

### Do you provide local expertise?

Yes. AfghanTours works through local guides, drivers, coordinators, and fixers throughout Afghanistan.

### Can routes change during the trip?

Yes. Weather, road conditions, checkpoints, and local coordination can require route changes.

### Can you support documentary projects?

Yes. AfghanTours supports journalists, filmmakers, photographers, and researchers.
