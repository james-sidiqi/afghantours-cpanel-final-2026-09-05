### Overview
Capital city, museums, markets, and modern Afghan life.

---

### Why Visit
Kabul is Afghanistan’s political and cultural center, combining historical neighborhoods, museums, gardens, and contemporary urban life. The city serves as the main gateway for travelers and the starting point for journeys across the country.

---

### Key Attractions
- Darulaman Palace
- Kabul National Museum
- Babur’s Gardens
- Shah-e-Du Shamshira Mosque
- Sakhi Shrine
- Wazir Akbar Khan Hill
- Chicken Street
- Khurd-Kabul Pass
