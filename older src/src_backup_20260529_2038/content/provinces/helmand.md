### Overview
River valleys, desert expanses, and ancient settlements.

---

### Why Visit
Helmand is shaped by the Helmand River, which sustains agriculture amid otherwise arid landscapes. The province has a deep history stretching back to ancient civilizations and remains vital to southern Afghanistan’s rural economy.

---

### Key Attractions
- Helmand River Landscape
- Qala-e-Bost Arch
