OVERVIEW

Provide a concise introduction explaining:

• What the attraction is
• Why it matters
• Why travelers visit
• What makes it distinctive
• How it fits into a wider Afghanistan journey

Target Length:
150–250 words

Writing Style:
Professional destination guide written by an experienced Kabul-based tour operator.

================================================================================
WHY VISIT

Explain the primary reasons travelers choose to visit.

Examples:

• Historical importance
• Cultural significance
• Architecture
• Landscapes
• Photography
• Archaeology
• Local traditions

Use short paragraphs or bullet points.

================================================================================
HISTORICAL & CULTURAL CONTEXT

Explain:

• Historical period
• Key events
• Cultural importance
• Architectural significance
• Local traditions

Remain factual and neutral.

Avoid political commentary.

Target Length:
200–500 words

================================================================================

AFGHANTOURS WRITING RULES

VOICE

Use:

• Professional
• Knowledgeable
• Historically grounded
• Operationally realistic
• Hospitality-focused

Avoid:

• Bucket-list language
• Adventure clichés
• Danger narratives
• Political commentary
• Travel-blog exaggeration

================================================================================
PRIMARY HUB RULE

primary_hub ALWAYS means:

“The overnight city used by travelers accessing this attraction.”

Examples:

Massoud Mausoleum
province: panjshir
primary_hub: kabul-city

Band-e-Amir
province: bamyan
primary_hub: bamyan-city

Jam Minaret
province: ghor
primary_hub: herat-city

Primary Hub DOES NOT mean:

• Nearest city
• Administrative center
• Closest town
• Provincial capital

It means the overnight hub used in AfghanTours itineraries.

================================================================================
GOAL

Every attraction page should read like it was written by an experienced
Afghanistan tour operator helping a traveler understand a place before visiting,
while remaining historically accurate, operationally realistic, and useful for
trip planning.
