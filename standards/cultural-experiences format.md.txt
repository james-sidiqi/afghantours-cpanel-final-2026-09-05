AFGHANTOURS CULTURAL EXPERIENCE WRITING RULES

VOICE

Use:

• Professional
• Educational
• Hospitality-focused
• Historically grounded
• Respectful

Avoid:

• Bucket-list language
• Exoticizing language
• Adventure clichés
• Political commentary
• Travel-blog exaggeration

================================================================================
GOAL

Every cultural experience page should help travelers understand not only
what they are seeing, but why it matters within Afghan society, history,
hospitality, and everyday life.

The page should feel like a knowledgeable local guide is introducing
the traveler to an authentic aspect of Afghanistan.
