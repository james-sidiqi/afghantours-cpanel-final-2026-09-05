OVERVIEW

Provide a comprehensive introduction explaining:

• What this tour is
• Why this itinerary exists
• What makes it unique
• Who it is designed for
• What regions are visited
• What experiences are included

Target Length:

400–800 words

The overview should tell the story of the journey rather than simply listing destinations.

================================================================================
WHO THIS TOUR IS FOR

Describe ideal travelers.

Examples:

• First-time visitors to Afghanistan
• Cultural travelers
• History enthusiasts
• Photographers
• Researchers
• Adventure travelers
• Returning veterans
• Expedition travelers

Explain why the itinerary suits them.

================================================================================
TOUR HIGHLIGHTS

List the major highlights.

Examples:

• Babur’s Gardens
• Band-e-Amir National Park
• Herat Citadel
• Blue Mosque
• Panjshir Valley
• Minaret of Jam

Focus on experiences rather than simply locations.

================================================================================
ROUTE OVERVIEW

Provide a narrative overview of the route.

Explain:

• Where the tour begins
• Major hubs used
• Key regions crossed
• How transportation works
• Why the route is designed this way

Avoid day-by-day detail.

The day-by-day itinerary handles that separately.

================================================================================
ACCOMMODATION STYLE

Describe:

• Typical hotel standard
• Room expectations
• Location style
• Guesthouses if applicable
• Upgrade options if available

Examples:

3-star and 4-star hotels in Kabul, Herat, and Bamyan.

Premium and luxury upgrades may be available at additional cost depending on city and availability.

Avoid guaranteeing specific hotels.

================================================================================
FOOD & CULTURE

Describe:

• Typical meals
• Regional specialties
• Tea houses
• Markets
• Cultural experiences

Examples:

• Qabuli Palaw
• Ashak
• Mantu
• Chai Khana culture
• Traditional bakeries
• Local restaurants

Focus on cultural immersion rather than luxury dining.

================================================================================
TRANSPORTATION

Describe:

• Domestic flights
• 4WD vehicles
• Highway travel
• Mountain roads
• Airport transfers

Explain transportation realities honestly.

Avoid overpromising.

================================================================================
PHOTOGRAPHY OPPORTUNITIES

Describe:

• Landscapes
• Architecture
• Markets
• Daily life
• Seasonal conditions
• Sunrise and sunset opportunities

Especially important for featured and photography tours.

================================================================================
BEST TIME TO TRAVEL

Explain:

• Seasons
• Weather
• Landscape conditions
• Festivals
• Road access

Provide realistic expectations.

AFGHANTOURS TOUR WRITING RULES

VOICE

Use:

• Professional
• Experienced
• Historically grounded
• Hospitality-focused
• Operationally realistic

Avoid:

• Bucket-list language
• Adventure clichés
• Political commentary
• Travel-blog exaggeration
• Safety guarantees

================================================================================
PRIMARY HUB RULE

primary_hub ALWAYS means:

“The primary overnight city used during the tour.”

Examples:

Weekend in Kabul
primary_hub: kabul-city

Timurid Grandeur Herat Tour
primary_hub: herat-city

Bamyan Skiing Tour
primary_hub: bamyan-city

================================================================================
OVERNIGHT HUB RULE

overnight_hubs MUST represent:

Cities where travelers actually sleep during the itinerary.

Examples:

overnight_hubs:

	•	kabul-city
	•	bamyan-city
	•	herat-city

NOT:

• Attractions
• Provinces
• Day trip destinations

• Restaurant
• Culture

• Transportation

• Attractions

================================================================================
GOAL

Every tour page should feel like a professionally designed expedition and
cultural journey rather than a simple travel package. The focus should remain on
history, culture, hospitality, landscapes, and realistic travel experiences
through Afghanistan.
