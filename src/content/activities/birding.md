Birding can be incorporated naturally into many AfghanTours itineraries.

Travel through Afghanistan passes through mountain valleys, wetlands, agricultural areas and other landscapes where travelers may have opportunities to observe local birdlife. If birding is one of several interests, tell us in advance and our guides can allow additional time for observation and photography where practical.

For dedicated birders looking for particular species, habitats or regions, a private itinerary is usually the better option. AfghanTours can design a [Custom Journey](/custom-requests/) around the areas and seasons most relevant to the traveler’s interests while coordinating the transportation and local access required.

Travelers should bring specialized binoculars, cameras or other personal birding equipment they prefer to use.

## Planning options

* Add birding time to most existing tours
* Bring your own optics and photography equipment
* Request additional observation time in relevant areas
* Plan a private itinerary around particular regions, habitats or target species
