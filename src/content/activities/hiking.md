Hiking in Afghanistan is planned from operational travel hubs, not as a freestanding catalog of named trailheads with guaranteed schedules.

## How it works

From each active hub, day hikes and viewpoint walks can be scoped around current road access, weather, and local guidance. Routes vary by region — highland valleys near Bamyan, foothill walks around Kabul, and corridor day options from other hubs when conditions allow.

## What we do not invent

AfghanTours does not publish invented trail names, timed summit guarantees, or fixed package prices on this page. Specific outing details are confirmed during planning.

## Related travel

Hiking often pairs with sightseeing days and, in the central highlands, with longer Bamyan stays. Multi-day expedition walking belongs under Trekking when staged from Faizabad.
