Trekking starts from **Faizabad**, the gateway to Badakhshan's high valleys and the road towards the Wakhan.

## Your trek, your pace

Every trek is planned around you:

- how much time you have
- difficulty and your group's experience
- season and weather
- route and road access
- the overnight and support options available

## How planning works

Tell us your dates and comfort level. Our Kabul team will reply with options — shorter highland days, longer journeys through the valleys, or a plan that mixes mountain walking with rest days.

## Mountaineering

For technical climbs such as Noshaq, our Specialist Services team handles full expedition support.
