Trekking is arranged from **Faizabad**, the Badakhshan logistics hub used for highland and corridor travel when access allows.

## Not one packaged expedition

This activity is not a single retail multi-day trek with a fixed day-by-day catalog, published trail list, or package price. Outings are scoped during planning around:

- how much time you have
- difficulty and group ability
- season and weather
- route and road access
- overnight and support options that are workable that week

## How planning works

Tell us your window and comfort level. We reply from Kabul with workable Faizabad-staged options — shorter highland days, longer corridor travel, or a proposal that mixes mountain movement with recovery days. Hub links mean we can **arrange from** Faizabad; they are not a tour inclusion list.

## Specialist mountaineering

Technical mountaineering support such as Noshaq expedition logistics remains under Specialist Services when required. This Activities page covers trekking as a hub-arranged experience class — it does not move specialist products into the experiences catalog.

## Related journeys

When a published itinerary explicitly features this activity, it appears under “Tours featuring this activity.” Otherwise, discuss trekking as part of custom or tour planning when routes are workable.
