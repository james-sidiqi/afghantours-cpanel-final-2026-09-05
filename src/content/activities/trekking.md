Trekking is staged from **Faizabad**, the Badakhshan logistics hub used for highland and corridor travel when access allows.

## Expedition posture

Multi-day trekking is proposal-led. Pace, overnight points, and support levels depend on season, weather, permits, and current route conditions. This is not a fixed retail trek with a published day-by-day itinerary or catalog price.

## Relationship to specialist services

Technical mountaineering support such as Noshaq expedition logistics remains under Specialist Services. This Activities page covers trekking as a hub-linked experience class — it does not move specialist products into the experiences catalog.

## Related journeys

Longer Badakhshan and Wakhan-oriented travel is discussed during custom and tour planning when routes are workable.
