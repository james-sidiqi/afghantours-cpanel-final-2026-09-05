Sightseeing is the heart of most Afghan journeys, wherever you travel.

## What you'll see

Historic monuments, shrines and mosques, scenic viewpoints and lively market streets. For shopping time, see our Shopping activity.

## Planning

Opening hours and access change with the season, so our team confirms the order of visits with you as we plan your days.
