Skiing is linked to the **Kabul** and **Bamyan** hubs for snow-season highland travel when conditions allow.

## Seasonal reality

Snow, road access, and local arrangements determine what is workable. AfghanTours does not invent resort names, lift tickets, or guaranteed powder days on this page.

## Specialist vs activity

Dedicated ski expedition support remains a Specialist Service. This Activities entry describes skiing as a hub-linked experience class available for journey planning — it does not replace or relocate the specialist service.

## Planning note

Bamyan’s highland winter reputation and Kabul-corridor snow access are the locked hubs for this activity. Exact outing details are confirmed during proposal planning.
