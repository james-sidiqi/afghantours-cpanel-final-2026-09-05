Cycling around **Kabul** is a relaxed way to see the city's quieter neighbourhoods and its edges, when traffic and road conditions are good.

## An easy-going ride

These are gentle, guided rides rather than mountain-bike expeditions — a different view of the city at an unhurried pace.

## How rides are planned

Bikes, routes and pricing are arranged with local guidance. Tell us you'd like to ride, and our team will plan the best option with you.

## More locations

We're adding rides in other parts of the country as we review local routes.
