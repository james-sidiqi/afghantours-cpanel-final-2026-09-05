### Traveler Responsibility

Travelers should remain flexible, punctual, respectful, and attentive to local guidance.

Follow guide and driver instructions during road movement and checkpoint interactions. Ask before taking photographs in sensitive settings, including government sites, checkpoints, military positions, infrastructure, religious sites, or when photographing people.

Travelers should understand that routes, timing, and activities may change because of weather, road conditions, local requirements, access, or other operating considerations.

Travelers should also read their own government's travel advisory and carry insurance that explicitly covers Afghanistan.
