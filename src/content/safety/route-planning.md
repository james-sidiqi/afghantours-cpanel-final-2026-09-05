### Route Planning

Routes are reviewed around road conditions, weather, daylight, seasonal access, practical drive times, and local requirements.

Long-distance movement is planned during daylight where practical. Mountain roads and remote routes can change quickly because of snow, flooding, construction, traffic, road damage, or temporary restrictions.

Alternative routes, overnight locations, or activity sequences may be used when the original plan is no longer practical.

The itinerary is a working operating plan, not a promise that every road will remain available.
