### Overview
Forested valleys, rivers, and dramatic eastern landscapes.

### Why Visit
Kunar is one of Afghanistan’s most scenic provinces, marked by steep valleys, flowing rivers, and dense forests. Its terrain supports unique ecosystems and offers opportunities for nature-focused travel.

### Key Attractions
- Kunar River Valley
- Asadabad Valley Views

### Travel Notes
- **Region:** East  
- **Best season:** Spring & Autumn (Summer warm; winters colder in higher valleys)
