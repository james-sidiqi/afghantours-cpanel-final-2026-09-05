### Overview

From Kabul the road follows the gorge until the cliffs close in and the Panjshir River runs beside you. Villages offer tea quickly.

The valley has a modern political history that travelers have heard of; we do not sell that history as a security rating, and we do not call the valley safe.

What we can plan is a day or two of landscape, conversation, and, when it makes sense, the emerald-working towns. Access is assessed before each departure.

### Key Attractions

- Ahmad Shah Massoud Mausoleum
- Paryan Valley

### Planning Panjshir

- **Best time:** May–June and September–October
- **Getting there:** Several hours by 4WD from Kabul when the road is open to the itinerary
- **Pace:** Light to moderate
- **Planning:** Conditions first. Panjshir is not automatically added to unrelated Kabul itineraries.
- **Permits:** Provincial tourist permits are processed in advance for booked tours
