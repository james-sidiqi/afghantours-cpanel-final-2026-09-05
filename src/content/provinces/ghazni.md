### Overview
Medieval Islamic history and highland trade routes.

---

### Why Visit
Once the capital of a powerful medieval empire, Ghazni is rich in Islamic architecture and historical significance. The province sits along historic trade routes linking Kabul with southern Afghanistan and the Indian subcontinent.
