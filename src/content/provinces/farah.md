### Overview
Desert landscapes and historic routes near the Iranian border.

### Why Visit
Farah features wide desert plains, historic caravan routes, and a strategic location along Afghanistan’s western frontier. The province reflects centuries of cross-border trade and movement between Afghanistan and the Iranian plateau.
