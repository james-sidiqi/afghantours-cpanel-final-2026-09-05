### Overview
Rolling hills, rural life, and historic routes between north and west.

### Why Visit
Badghis sits between Afghanistan’s northern plains and western highlands, known for its rolling terrain, pastoral communities, and historical importance as a transit region. Its landscapes and village life reflect a quieter, agrarian side of the country.

### Key Attractions
