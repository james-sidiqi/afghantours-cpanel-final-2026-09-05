### Overview
Historic valleys and archaeological sites south of Kabul.

### Why Visit
Logar lies just south of Kabul and features fertile valleys, historic villages, and archaeological remains from ancient civilizations. Its landscapes reflect long-standing settlement and agricultural traditions.
