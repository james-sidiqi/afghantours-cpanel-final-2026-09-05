### Overview
Northern steppe landscapes and cultural diversity.

### Why Visit
Jowzjan lies in the northern plains and is known for its agricultural lands and ethnically diverse communities. Historically connected to trade routes linking Central Asia with Afghanistan, the province reflects a blend of cultures and traditions.
