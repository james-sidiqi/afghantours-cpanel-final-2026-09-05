### Overview
Northern plains, Turkic heritage, and historic trade routes.

### Why Visit
Faryab is part of Afghanistan’s northern plains and is known for its diverse cultural heritage, particularly Turkic influences. The province’s open landscapes and market towns reflect its long role in regional trade and agriculture.
