### Overview

Ghor is not a city-break.

The Minaret of Jam stands about 65 meters above a river gorge — Ghurid brickwork from the late 12th century, still standing in a place that takes a serious 4WD day to reach.

Villages along the Hari Rud keep their own schedule. This is a seasonal expedition, not a standard featured-tour add-on.

### Key Attractions

- [Minaret of Jam](/attractions/minaret-of-jam/)
- Rural Ghor Highlands

### Planning Ghor & Jam

- **Season:** We do not run Jam or Ghor in winter.
- **Working window:** Spring and autumn are the preferred operating window; road conditions still determine access.
- **Transport:** Remote overland travel by 4WD
- **Pace:** Expedition-style travel with long road days
- **Permits:** Provincial tourist permits are processed in advance for booked tours
