### Overview

The valley sits high and open. The giant Buddhas are gone — destroyed in 2001 — and the niches are still the first thing you see. Hazara farming villages work the floor of the valley.

Band-e-Amir is a separate day when the road and weather allow it: mineral-blue lakes under the Hindu Kush.

Bamyan is slower than Kabul. Plan for altitude and for days that start early so you are not crossing mountain roads in the dark.

### Key Attractions

- Buddha Niches of Bamyan
- Shahr-e-Gholghola
- Shahr-e-Zohak
- Band-e-Amir National Park
- Koh-e-Baba Mountain Range
- Dragon Valley
- Chehel Burj Bamyan Ruins

### Planning Bamyan

- **Best time:** May–June and September–October
- **Getting there:** Overland from Kabul by 4WD, usually 8–10 hours. The road is chosen for conditions and schedule rather than fixed in advance.
- **Pace:** Moderate; altitude
- **Permits:** Provincial tourist permits are processed in advance for booked tours.
