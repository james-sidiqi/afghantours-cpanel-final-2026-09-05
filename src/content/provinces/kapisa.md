### Overview
Green valleys and ancient Buddhist remains near Kabul.

### Why Visit
Kapisa lies northeast of Kabul and is known for fertile valleys and archaeological remnants from Afghanistan’s Buddhist past. Its proximity to the capital makes it a natural extension of Kabul-region travel.

### Key Attractions
- Nijrab Valley
