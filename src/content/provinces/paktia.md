### Overview
Mountain valleys and strong tribal traditions.

### Why Visit
Paktia lies in southeastern Afghanistan and is defined by mountainous terrain and close-knit tribal communities. The province has a long history of autonomy shaped by its geography.

### Travel Notes
- **Region:** East  
- **Best season:** Spring & Autumn (Summer warm; winters colder in higher valleys)
