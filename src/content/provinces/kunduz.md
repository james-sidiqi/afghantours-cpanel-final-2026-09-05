### Overview
Northern river plains and historic Silk Road connections.

### Why Visit
Kunduz sits on fertile plains nourished by northern rivers and has long been an agricultural hub. Its location near historic Silk Road routes underscores its importance in trade and settlement.

### Key Attractions
- Kunduz City & Farmlands

### Travel Notes
- **Region:** North  
- **Best season:** Spring & Autumn (Summer can be hot in lowlands)
