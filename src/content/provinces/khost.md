### Overview
Mountain passes and tribal regions near the Pakistan border.

### Why Visit
Khost is a southeastern province defined by forested mountains and strong tribal traditions. Historically a gateway between Afghanistan and the Indian subcontinent, it reflects deep cultural continuity and strategic geography.
