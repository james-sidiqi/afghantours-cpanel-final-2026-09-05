### Overview

Kabul is not a museum. It is the city where Afghanistan works, argues, prays, and sells tea before sunrise. Older quarters sit against later palaces, Soviet monuments, and hillsides of houses.

Walk Chicken Street if you want the antique trade. Climb toward the Sakhi Shrine when you want the city in one view. Spend an hour in Babur’s Gardens when you want quiet.

The city rewards people who are patient with traffic and interested in ordinary life.

### Key Attractions
