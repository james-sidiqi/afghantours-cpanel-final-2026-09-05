<h2 id="scheduled-departures">3. Scheduled Departures</h2>

Unless a particular departure states otherwise:

Tours lasting 5 days or more

* minimum: 6 confirmed travelers
* normal maximum: 18 travelers

Tours lasting 4 days or less

* minimum: 2 confirmed travelers
* no standard maximum unless a specific capacity is stated.

A traveler counts toward the minimum only after the booking has been accepted and the required payment has been received.

An inquiry does not count.

A scheduled departure becomes Confirmed only when the applicable minimum has been reached and AfghanTours has given operational approval.

<h2 id="minimum-group-review">4. Minimum-Group Review</h2>

Scheduled/shared tours lasting 5 days or more are normally reviewed 45 days before departure.

Scheduled/shared tours lasting 4 days or less are normally reviewed 14 days before departure.

These are review points, not automatic cancellation dates.

If the applicable minimum or operational approval requirement has not been satisfied, AfghanTours may:

* approve operation as an exception;
* offer another scheduled departure;
* offer an alternative itinerary;
* offer a separately quoted private arrangement; or
* cancel the scheduled departure.

<h2 id="late-bookings">5. Late Bookings</h2>

Confirmed scheduled departures may continue accepting travelers after the normal review point, subject to availability and operational feasibility.

Confirmed tours lasting 5 days or more may remain available inside 30 days before departure.

During the final 7 days, normal online selection may be replaced by:

Contact us for last-minute availability

Last-minute requests require manual confirmation.
