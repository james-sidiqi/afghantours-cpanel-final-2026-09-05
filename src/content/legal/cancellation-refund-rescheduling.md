## Scheduled Departures and Minimum Group Size {#scheduled-departures-minimum-group-size}

Some published tours operate as scheduled group departures and require a minimum number of confirmed travelers before the departure is guaranteed to operate.

Unless otherwise stated for a specific departure, scheduled group tours lasting five days or more require a minimum of six confirmed travelers and normally accept a maximum of eighteen travelers. Scheduled or shared departures lasting four days or less require a minimum of two confirmed travelers. The minimum that applies to your departure is stated in the tour information or in your booking confirmation.

A traveler's individual booking may be confirmed while the scheduled departure remains open and awaiting the required minimum number of travelers. AfghanTours will advise booked travelers when the departure becomes confirmed.

If the required minimum is not reached, James Tourist & Travel Agency may offer another scheduled departure, an alternative itinerary, a private version of the journey at a separately quoted price, or cancellation of the scheduled departure.

If James Tourist & Travel Agency cancels a scheduled departure because the required minimum number of travelers has not been reached, it will refund 100% of the amount paid to James Tourist & Travel Agency for that tour.

This does not include independently purchased airfare, visas, insurance, accommodation, transportation, or other services not purchased from James Tourist & Travel Agency.

The standard maximum of eighteen travelers may be adjusted for specially arranged groups or other approved departures. Travelers, organizations, or trade partners seeking an exception should contact AfghanTours.

Tours sold as private journeys are not subject to the scheduled-group minimum unless specifically stated in the applicable proposal or booking confirmation. Private pricing may differ from advertised shared-departure pricing.

## Payments, Currency and Refunds {#payments-currency-refunds}

Prices may be quoted in US dollars or in another currency stated in your quotation or booking confirmation.

Some payment providers process payments in Afghani (AFN) or another settlement currency. Where a refund is made, the amount you receive may be affected by exchange-rate movements, and by bank, card, currency-conversion, or payment-provider fees.

An approved refund is calculated under the policy that applies to your booking and is not automatically adjusted for exchange-rate changes between the date of payment and the date of refund.

Details of the payment method and currency used for your booking are given at checkout or in your booking confirmation.
