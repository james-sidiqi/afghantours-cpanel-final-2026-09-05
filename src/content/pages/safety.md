### Your safety is planned, not promised.

Travel in Afghanistan carries inherent risk. AfghanTours does not promise that a destination is safe or that conditions will remain unchanged.

Our job is to plan around the conditions we can assess: route access, road movement, weather, daylight, local coordination, documentation requirements, practical overnight points, and alternatives when the original plan no longer makes sense.

### Route Planning

Routes are reviewed around real drive times, road conditions, seasonal access, weather, daylight, and the requirements of the itinerary.

A planned road, activity, or destination can change when conditions change.

### Local Coordination

Travel is supported through Kabul-based planning and working relationships with guides, drivers, hotels, and local contacts relevant to the itinerary.

Access is assessed before movement rather than assumed.

### Letter of Invitation, Visas & Permits

Letter of Invitation support is included for every booked tour.

AfghanTours does not issue visas. Visa decisions remain with the relevant authorities.

Provincial permissions and registrations are processed or coordinated in advance where required. Exact requirements depend on the itinerary and the authorities responsible for the destination.

### Road Movement

Vehicles and drivers are selected according to terrain, distance, group size, and route needs.

Daylight movement is preferred where practical. Timing or routing can change because of weather, road conditions, checkpoints, local access, or other operating considerations.

### Checkpoints & Photography

Travelers should follow guide instructions during checkpoint interactions.

Ask before photographing government sites, checkpoints, military positions, infrastructure, religious sites, or people in sensitive settings.

### Insurance & Medical Planning

Travelers should read their own government's travel advisory and carry travel insurance that explicitly covers Afghanistan.

Emergency evacuation coverage is strongly recommended. Medical capability and response options vary considerably between Kabul, provincial cities, and remote areas.

### Traveler Responsibility

Travelers are expected to respect Afghan law and customs, cooperate with guides and drivers, and accept itinerary changes when conditions require them.
