# Experience Afghanistan

Not the headlines. The hospitality. The history. The highlands.

AfghanTours is a licensed, American-led tour operator based in Kabul. We plan private journeys with local guides, Letter of Invitation support for every booked tour, and routes that can change when the road, the weather, or access changes.


## Trust

Licensed Afghan Tour Operator · American-led, Kabul-based · 15+ years on the ground · Private local guides · Letter of Invitation support on every booked tour

## Why travel with AfghanTours

Afghanistan is extraordinary. It deserves more than guesswork.

### On the ground in Kabul

Journeys are planned from Kabul around real routes, seasons, access, and operating conditions. Drivers, guides, and hotel partners are people we already work with.

### Letter of Invitation

Every customer who books a tour with us receives LOI support. Provincial travel permits for the itinerary are processed in advance. We do not issue visas.

### Routes that can change

Itineraries are built around real drive times, seasons, and access. If a pass is the wrong call that week, we use another road or we wait.

### Vehicles

Standard journeys use 4WD. An armored vehicle is available as a premium option when you request it. It is not required.

### What the days are for

Markets, tea houses, working monuments, and the people who keep them. Not a checklist of ruins.
