### 🔐 PRIVACY POLICY

AfghanTours is committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining confidentiality in all client interactions.

---

### 📌 INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect:
- Full name
- Passport details
- Contact information (email, phone, WhatsApp)
- Travel preferences and itinerary details

This information is collected solely for the purpose of providing travel services.

---

### 🗂️ USE OF INFORMATION

Your data is used to:
- Arrange travel permits and registrations
- Coordinate with hotels and transportation providers
- Communicate itinerary details and updates
- Provide emergency assistance if required

---

### 🤝 DATA SHARING

Information is shared **only when necessary** with:
- Government authorities (for permits)
- Accommodation providers
- Transportation partners

We do not sell or share personal data for marketing purposes.

---

### 🔒 DATA SECURITY

- Digital records are stored securely
- Access is limited to authorized personnel
- Physical copies are handled with discretion during travel

---

### ⏳ DATA RETENTION

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to:
- Complete services
- Meet legal or administrative requirements

---

### 📧 COMMUNICATION

Primary communication is conducted via:
- WhatsApp
- Email

Clients may request deletion of their data after completion of services, subject to legal obligations.

---

### 📞 CONTACT

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us directly using the details provided on the Contact page.
