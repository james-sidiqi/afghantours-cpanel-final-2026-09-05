### Frequently Asked Questions

### 🛂 General Travel & Logistics

### Do I need a visa to visit Afghanistan?
Yes. All foreign nationals require a tourist visa, typically issued for 30 days. Visas must be obtained from an Afghan embassy or consulate before arrival.

### Is it safe to travel to Afghanistan right now?
Security conditions vary by region. We operate with proactive planning, real-time local updates, adaptive itineraries, and embassy registration guidance where appropriate.

### What is the best time of year to visit?
Spring, from April to June, and autumn, from September to November, generally offer the best conditions.

### How do I get around between provinces?
Domestic flights through Kabul and private ground transport are used depending on route, season, timing, and current road conditions.

### Are travel permits required?
Yes. AfghanTours assists with local coordination and required permissions as part of the planning process.

### 🕌 Cultural Etiquette

### What should I wear in Afghanistan?
Modest dress is required. Men should avoid shorts in public. Women should wear loose-fitting clothing and carry a headscarf.

### Can I take photographs freely?
Photography restrictions apply near checkpoints, government buildings, officials, military sites, and sensitive locations. Your guide will advise when photography is appropriate.

### 🛌 Booking & Payments

### How are hotel rooms charged?
Couples sharing a room are charged one room rate. Solo travelers or travelers requesting separate rooms are charged according to the room type and number of rooms required.

### How do I pay for a tour?
Cash payment in USD is generally required. Final payment instructions are provided during booking confirmation.

### Are prices final?
Prices are confirmed after route review, hotel availability, transport requirements, permits, and current operating conditions are checked.

### 🍲 Food & Health

### Is the food safe?
We use trusted restaurants and hotels where possible. Travelers should drink bottled water only and follow guide advice on food selection.

### Are vegetarian options available?
Yes. Vegetarian options are usually available, especially in major cities. Please tell us dietary needs before travel.

### 📱 Connectivity

### Can I get a local SIM card?
Yes. Local SIM cards are available, and AfghanTours can assist with basic guidance after arrival.

### Is internet available?
Internet is generally available in major cities, but speed and reliability vary. Remote areas may have limited or no reliable service.

### 🛡️ Safety & Emergencies

### What happens at checkpoints?
Your guide manages checkpoint communication and will advise you when to keep documents ready, when to avoid photography, and how to proceed respectfully.

### What happens in an emergency?
Emergency contacts and local support procedures are provided after booking. Your guide and operations team coordinate the immediate response based on the location and situation.

### 🧭 Custom Tours

### Can AfghanTours arrange custom trips?
Yes. We arrange custom travel for diaspora visitors, veterans, photographers, journalists, researchers, mountain sports travelers, overlanding groups, and private cultural tours.

### Can I request a route that is not listed on the website?
Yes. Some routes require additional review for road conditions, access, permissions, seasonality, and security before we can confirm feasibility.
