### Fixed vs Custom Tours

AfghanTours offers both structured featured tours and custom-designed itineraries.

Fixed tours provide a strong starting framework. Custom tours allow routes, timing, hotels, transport, and experiences to be adjusted around traveler goals and operational feasibility.

### Fixed Tours

Fixed or featured tours are best for travelers who want a proven route, clear structure, and a realistic overview of what the trip includes.

These tours usually include:
- planned route
- estimated duration
- key attractions
- standard hotel assumptions
- vehicle planning
- guide support
- starting price or estimated price

Fixed tours are still reviewed before confirmation because conditions in Afghanistan can change.

### Custom Tours

Custom tours are designed around the traveler’s goals.

### Which Is Better?

Choose a fixed tour if you want a clear, efficient route.

Choose a custom tour if you want a trip built around your exact interests, pace, comfort level, and destination priorities.
