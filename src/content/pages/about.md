# Not the headlines. The hospitality.

American-led, Kabul-based, and working inside the country.

AfghanTours is owned and operated by **James Tourist & Travel Agency**, a licensed Afghan tour operator in Kabul.

**License:** ATO-KBL-1617

Leadership is American-led and the team is Afghan. President / Co-Founder James “Amir” Sidiqi and Vice President / Co-Founder Mohammad “Edrees” Rahimi run the company from Kabul.

We plan tourism, documentary work, expeditions, and related logistics with people we already know. We do not parachute in for the season.

## How we think about safety

The question everyone asks is whether a trip is “safe.” We will not answer that with a guarantee.

We answer it with routing, local coordination, and a willingness to change the day.


## Letter of Invitation and permits

Letter of Invitation support is part of every booked tour.

Provincial travel permits for the itinerary are processed in advance.

AfghanTours does not issue visas.

Travelers should read their own government’s travel advisory and arrange insurance that covers Afghanistan.
