### Overview
Taj Continental Hotel is a standard accommodation option used for AfghanTours itineraries in Ghazni City. It is selected for its practical location, traveler suitability, and ability to support guided travel operations in Afghanistan.

A clean room with AC and heating. Great food prepared at on-site restaurant.

### Accommodation Style

Available room configurations include two doubles. Room allocation depends on group size, traveler preference, seasonal availability, and confirmed pricing at the time of booking.

### Operational Notes

Hotel standards in Afghanistan should be understood in practical local context. Properties are selected for cleanliness, comfort, access, and operational reliability rather than generic international resort expectations.

Electricity, internet, heating, cooling, and hot water can vary by city and season. Where relevant, AfghanTours confirms hotel suitability before finalizing the itinerary.
