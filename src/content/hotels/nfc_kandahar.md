### Overview
NFC Hotel is a standard accommodation option used for AfghanTours itineraries in Kandahar City. It is selected for its practical location, traveler suitability, and ability to support guided travel operations in Afghanistan.

Modern hotel complex with restaurant, snooker club, indoor pool and gymnasium facilities in the heart of Aino Mina.

### Accommodation Style

Available room configurations include quad twins, single twin, suite, two twins. Room allocation depends on group size, traveler preference, seasonal availability, and confirmed pricing at the time of booking.

### Operational Notes

Hotel standards in Afghanistan should be understood in practical local context. Properties are selected for cleanliness, comfort, access, and operational reliability rather than generic international resort expectations.

Electricity, internet, heating, cooling, and hot water can vary by city and season. Where relevant, AfghanTours confirms hotel suitability before finalizing the itinerary.

### Location Context

This hotel supports itineraries operating through Kandahar City. It may be used as an overnight base, arrival/departure hotel, or regional staging point depending on the route.
