### Short Introduction
The high-altitude gateway to the north. Traversing the Hindu Kush at over 3,400 meters, the
Salang Pass is one of the world's most dramatic mountain crossings. The route is defined by
steep switchbacks, massive snow galleries, and the engineering marvel of the Salang Tunnel,
offering rugged scenery and crisp alpine air.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Salang Pass is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built monument.
It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape travel through
Parwan and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
