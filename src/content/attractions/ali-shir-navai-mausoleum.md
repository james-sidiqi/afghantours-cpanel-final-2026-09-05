## Mausoleum of Mir Ali-Shir Nava'i

The mausoleum of Mir Ali-Shir Nava'i — also spelled Ali Sher Nava'i — stands at the Musalla complex in Herat, near the remaining minarets and the tomb of Gohar Shad.

See the [Musalla Complex cultural experience](/cultural-experiences/musalla-complex/).
