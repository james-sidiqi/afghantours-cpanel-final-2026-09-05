### Short Introduction
Dramatic mountain pass of military legend. This narrow gorge southeast of Kabul was the site of
significant 19th-century battles. Its towering cliffs and winding stream offer a rugged natural
beauty and a physical link to the 'Great Game' history that shaped the region.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Khurd-Kabul Pass is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built
monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape
travel through Kabul and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 3-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
