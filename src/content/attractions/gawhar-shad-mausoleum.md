## Gawhar Shad Mausoleum

The tomb of Gohar Shad — also spelled Gawhar Shad — stands at the Musalla complex in Herat, beside the remaining minarets. It is part of the same visit.

See the [Musalla Complex cultural experience](/cultural-experiences/musalla-complex/) and the [Herat Minarets](/attractions/herat-minarets-musalla-complex/).
