### Short Introduction
Site of the famous 'Bactrian Gold'. Known as the 'Hill of Gold,' this archaeological site near
Sheberghan was the discovery point of over 20,000 pieces of ancient gold jewelry. It highlights
northern Afghanistan's role as a rich crossroads where Greek, Scythian, and Chinese cultures
met 2,000 years ago.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Jowzjan, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 3-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
