## Khwaja Abdullah Ansari Shrine (Gazargah)

The shrine of the Sufi scholar Khwaja Abdullah Ansari stands at Gazargah, on the edge of Herat. It is one of the city's important places of pilgrimage and memory. We usually plan it with the rest of your time in Herat.

## Planning a visit

The shrine is a living place of prayer and burial. Visitors should move quietly, dress conservatively, and follow local guidance around the courtyard, graves, and photography.
