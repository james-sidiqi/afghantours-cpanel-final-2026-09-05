### Short Introduction
The most revered spiritual site in Kandahar. This mosque houses the 'Kharqa Sharif', a cloak
believed to have been worn by the Prophet Muhammad. It is the spiritual epicenter of the south,
known for its intricate tilework and the deep reverence it commands from pilgrims across the
nation.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The site should be approached as a living place of memory and respect. Visitors should move
quietly, dress conservatively, and follow local guidance around prayer spaces, graves,
caretakers, and photography.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable
pace for walking.
