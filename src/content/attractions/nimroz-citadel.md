### Short Introduction
A monumental mud-brick desert ruin. Located in the Chakhansur district, this massive citadel
was once the seat of power in the Sistan basin. Built from thousands of sun-dried bricks, its
ruins rise dramatically from the flat desert plains, testifying to a 2,000-year history of
power and survival.

### Cultural / Historical Context
This is a history-focused stop where the surrounding setting is part of the experience. Ruins,
surviving structures, and local place memory help visitors understand how older political,
religious, and trade networks shaped this part of Afghanistan.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
