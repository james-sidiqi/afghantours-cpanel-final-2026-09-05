### Short Introduction
The historic 'Mother of Cities'. As one of the oldest cities in the world, Balkh (ancient
Bactria) was a spiritual center for Zoroastrianism and Buddhism long before the Islamic era.
Visitors can explore massive sun-dried mud-brick walls, the ruins of the Green Mosque, and
layers of history that define the Silk Road.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Balkh, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 3-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable
pace for walking.

### Best For
- Travelers interested in silkroad
- History-focused travelers
- Travelers building a wider regional itinerary
