## Abu Nasr Khwaja Parsa Shrine

Abu Nasr Khwaja Parsa Shrine is also known as the Green Mosque or Rauza-e-Sharif. Visit it as part of the [Ancient City of Balkh cultural experience](/cultural-experiences/ancient-city-of-balkh/).

Approach the shrine respectfully and follow local guidance around prayer spaces and photography.
