### Short Introduction
Striking silhouettes of a lost empire. The remaining minarets of the Musalla complex are a
haunting reminder of the city's past as a center of the Timurid Renaissance. These towering
brick structures, once part of a massive madrasa and mosque complex, offer one of Afghanistan's
most iconic sunset views.

### Cultural / Historical Context
This is a history-focused stop where the surrounding setting is part of the experience. Ruins,
surviving structures, and local place memory help visitors understand how older political,
religious, and trade networks shaped this part of Afghanistan.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable
pace for walking.
