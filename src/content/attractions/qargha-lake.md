### Short Introduction
Kabul's favorite weekend lakeside escape. Just a short drive from the city center, this
reservoir is the primary recreation spot for Kabulis. With boat rides, lakeside restaurants,
and fresh mountain air, it provides a relaxed, social atmosphere and a different side of life
in the capital.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Qargha Lake is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built monument.
It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape travel through
Kabul and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Winter access may be
limited; confirm conditions before travel.
