### Short Introduction
Kabul's legendary antiques market. A historic corridor for travelers, Chicken Street is the go-
to place for lapis lazuli jewelry, hand-woven carpets, and traditional Afghan crafts. Even for
non-shoppers, it offers a fascinating look at the country's diverse artisanal heritage.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The value of the stop is not only architectural. It is also a window into local trade, street
life, craft culture, and the everyday rhythm of the surrounding city.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.

### Best For
- Travelers interested in antiques
- Travelers interested in shopping
- Travelers interested in local markets and craft culture
- Travelers building a wider regional itinerary
