### Short Introduction
Vast rolling hills and pastoral plains. Sar-e-Pol is a region of immense, open meadows that
serve as a corridor for nomadic movement in the north. The landscape is characterized by its
scale and simplicity, offering wide horizons and a peaceful view of Afghanistan's northern
pastoral life.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Sar-e-Pol Meadows is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built
monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape
travel through Sar-e-Pol and the wider North region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
