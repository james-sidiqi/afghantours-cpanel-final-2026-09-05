### Short Introduction
Nature's petrified 'Dragon' ridge. Named after a distinctive rock formation that resembles a
petrified dragon, this valley is steeped in local folklore. A walk along the 'spine' of the
dragon reveals mineral springs and unique geological textures, making it a quick and rewarding
nature excursion near Bamyan.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Dragon Valley is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built
monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape
travel through Bamyan and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
