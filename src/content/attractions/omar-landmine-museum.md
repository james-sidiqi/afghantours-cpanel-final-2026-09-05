### Short Introduction
Educational insight into humanitarian work. This unique museum provides a vital look at the
technical side of demining. Through its collection of hardware and instructional displays, it
honors the deminers who have made Afghanistan safer and offers a perspective on the challenges
of post-conflict recovery.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Kabul, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.

### Best For
- Travelers interested in education
- Travelers interested in humanitarian
- History-focused travelers
- Travelers building a wider regional itinerary
