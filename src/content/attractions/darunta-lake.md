### Short Introduction
Scenic reservoir with a mountain backdrop. Darunta Lake is a popular recreation spot near
Jalalabad. Framed by rugged hills, the lake offers a peaceful waterfront setting for boat rides
and riverside dining, serving as a vital irrigation source for the eastern plains.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Darunta Lake is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built
monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape
travel through Nangarhar and the wider East region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Winter access may be
limited; confirm conditions before travel.
