### Short Introduction
A hilltop steeped in 'Great Game' history. Gandamak is famously known as the site of the final
stand of the British 44th Regiment during the 1842 retreat from Kabul. Today, the quiet hilltop
provides a place to reflect on the immense scale of history that has passed through these
eastern passes.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Gandamak Last Stand Site is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a
built monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that
shape travel through Nangarhar and the wider East region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 3-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
