### Short Introduction
Traditional dwellings of the southwest. This village site near Zaranj offers a rare look at the
unique desert architecture of southwestern Afghanistan. The use of wind-towers and thick mud
walls showcases how local communities have adapted to the extreme heat of the Sistan desert for
centuries.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Nimroz, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
