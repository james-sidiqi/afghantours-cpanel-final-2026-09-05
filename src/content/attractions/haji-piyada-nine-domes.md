### Short Introduction
Afghanistan's oldest surviving mosque. Dating back to the 9th century, this humble yet
historically vital structure is a rare example of early Samanid-style architecture. Its
remaining columns and exquisite carved stucco work offer a quiet, profound glimpse into the
early Islamic artistic traditions of Central Asia.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Balkh, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable
pace for walking.
