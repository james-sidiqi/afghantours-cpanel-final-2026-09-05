### Short Introduction
A vivid blue beacon of Kabul's faith. Nestled at the foot of Mount Asmai, this major shrine is
famous for its vibrant blue and green tiles. It is a central place of worship for the city's
Shi'a community and a peaceful space that offers a profound look at living religious traditions
in the capital.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The site should be approached as a living place of memory and respect. Visitors should move
quietly, dress conservatively, and follow local guidance around prayer spaces, graves,
caretakers, and photography.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light
for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
