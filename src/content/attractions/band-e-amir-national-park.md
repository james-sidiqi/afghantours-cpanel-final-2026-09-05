### Short Introduction
Stunning sapphire-blue travertine lakes. Often called 'The Grand Canyon of Afghanistan,' this
national park features six natural dams made of travertine. The contrast between the deep,
electric-blue water and the towering, sun-scorched limestone cliffs creates one of the most
breathtaking natural spectacles in the world.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Band-e-Amir National Park is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a
built monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that
shape travel through Bamyan and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 8-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Winter access may be
limited; confirm conditions before travel.
