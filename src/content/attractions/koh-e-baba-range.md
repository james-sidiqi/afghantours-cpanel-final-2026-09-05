### Short Introduction
The snowy spine of the Central Highlands. Part of the Hindu Kush system, the Koh-e-Baba range
is the watershed of Afghanistan. It offers rugged alpine beauty for hikers in the summer and a
growing destination for winter sports, with peaks providing a stark, beautiful backdrop to the
Bamyan Valley.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Koh-e-Baba Mountain Range is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a
built monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that
shape travel through Bamyan and the wider Central region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
