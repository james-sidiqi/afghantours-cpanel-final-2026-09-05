## Jahan Nama Palace

The Jahan Nama palace — also called Bagh-e Jahan Nama — stands in Khulm (Tashqurghan), east of Mazar-e-Sharif. It is a late-nineteenth-century palace and garden. We usually visit it from Mazar, according to access and conditions on the day.

The local team coordinates the visit according to access and conditions on the day.
