### Short Introduction
A vibrant maze of silk and copper. Herat's Old City is a living museum. Within its narrow
alleys, you'll find ancient silk-weaving workshops, copper-smiths hammering by hand, and spice
markets that have remained active for centuries, maintaining the authentic rhythm of a Silk
Road trading hub.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The value of the stop is not only architectural. It is also a window into local trade, street
life, craft culture, and the everyday rhythm of the surrounding city.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access is generally straightforward by Afghan travel standards,
but timing still depends on local coordination, road conditions, and the day’s operating plan.
Morning and late afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable
pace for walking.

### Best For
- Travelers interested in artisans
- Travelers interested in silkroad
- Travelers interested in photography
- Travelers building a wider regional itinerary
