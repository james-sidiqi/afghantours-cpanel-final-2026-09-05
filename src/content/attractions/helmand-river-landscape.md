### Short Introduction
A lush desert lifeline and river oasis. The Helmand River creates a striking green ribbon
through the arid southern plains. Along its banks, fertile fields of wheat and fruit orchards
provide a vibrant contrast to the surrounding desert, showing how water has sustained life here
for millennia.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Helmand River Landscape is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a
built monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that
shape travel through Helmand and the wider South region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 4-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.
