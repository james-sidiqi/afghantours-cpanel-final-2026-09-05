### Short Introduction
Turkmen heritage and artisan weaving. Faryab's villages are the heart of Afghanistan's northern
carpet-weaving traditions. Visitors can experience the unique Turkmen culture, traditional
yurt-style homes, and vibrant local markets where world-class hand-knotted rugs have been
traded for generations.

### Cultural / Historical Context
The attraction adds regional context to a journey through Faryab, connecting the visitor
experience to local history, landscape, and daily life rather than functioning as a stand-alone
sightseeing stop only.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.

### Best For
- Travelers interested in carpets
- Travelers interested in turkmenculture
- Travelers interested in artisans
