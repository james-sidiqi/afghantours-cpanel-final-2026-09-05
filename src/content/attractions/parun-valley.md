### Short Introduction
The capital of 'The Land of Light'. Parun is the heart of Nuristan, a region of incredible
isolation and unique wooden architecture. The valley is famous for its intricate timber-framed
homes and a culture that remained distinct for centuries, set within a landscape of dense
forests and rushing rivers.

### Cultural / Historical Context
For travelers, Parun Valley is best understood as a landscape stop rather than a built
monument. It helps show the terrain, settlement patterns, and seasonal movement that shape
travel through Nuristan and the wider East region.

### Visitor Reality
Plan this as a 2-hour visit. Access may involve uneven ground, longer driving, or more careful
timing than a simple city stop. The preferred movement style is road. Morning and late
afternoon usually give the best light for photography and a more comfortable pace for walking.

- Travelers interested in nuristan
