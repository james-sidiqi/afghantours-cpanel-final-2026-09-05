## Overview

Media Support is built for journalists, broadcasters, photographers, documentary teams, and production crews that need practical local coordination in Afghanistan. The service is designed around the assignment rather than a predetermined tourism itinerary.

## Support Can Include

- Airport, hotel, vehicle, and driver coordination
- Local fixer and interpretation requirements
- Location research and route planning
- Interview and appointment coordination where contacts are available
- Field movement between Kabul and provincial locations
- Support with understanding local permit or access requirements
- Production logistics, schedule changes, and equipment movement planning
- Optional cultural or historical context relevant to the assignment

## Who This Service Is For

News organizations, independent journalists, documentary producers, photographers, researchers, film crews, and other professional media teams working to a defined brief.

## Assignment Planning

Send us the assignment dates, team size, equipment profile, intended locations, subjects, interview requirements, and expected outputs. We use that information to build a practical operating plan and identify matters that require confirmation before travel.

## Permits, Access & Conditions

Permissions and access requirements vary by location, subject, activity, and current local rules. AfghanTours can assist with local coordination and planning, but access or official approval is always subject to the responsible authority or host.

## Accommodation & Transport

Transport and hotels are selected around the production schedule. Domestic flights and 4WD vehicles may be incorporated when they are available and appropriate to the assignment.

## Request Media Support

This service is quoted individually. Send us your brief and proposed dates so that we can scope the required support.
