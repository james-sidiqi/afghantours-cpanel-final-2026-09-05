### Scientific Expeditions

Scientific Expeditions provide custom field logistics for researchers, academics, scientific teams, and documentary projects working in Afghanistan. The route is built around the research objective, required field sites, access requirements, equipment, and available time.

### What Support Can Include

- Arrival, hotels, vehicles, drivers, and domestic movement
- Field-route planning and logistics
- Interpretation and local coordination
- Equipment movement and practical resupply planning
- Scheduling around research sites and local appointments
- Support in identifying access or permission requirements
- Contingency days for weather, roads, flight changes, or fieldwork delays

### 14-Day Planning Framework

The published 14-day schedule should be read as a sample field-support framework, not a promise that every scientific expedition follows the same cities or sites. Final field sectors are selected from the research brief.

### Planning Notes

Before confirming a program, AfghanTours reviews the project purpose, team composition, equipment, required locations, timing, transport needs, access considerations, and any host or institutional coordination already in place. Final routing and pricing are then proposed for the specific assignment.
