## Overview

Veteran Return is a private, individually planned journey for former military personnel who want to revisit Afghanistan, see places connected to their service, and understand how the country and its communities have changed.

## A Journey Built Around Your History

There is no standard Veteran Return itinerary. The route starts with the locations that matter to you: former duty areas, cities, roads, landscapes, or communities associated with your time in Afghanistan. We then assess what can realistically be included and build the journey around those priorities.

## What We Can Coordinate

- Private arrival, hotel, vehicle, and driver arrangements
- Route planning around personally significant locations
- Local guides and cultural context
- Visits to public locations and communities where practical
- Time for photography, reflection, and understanding present-day life
- Optional heritage and cultural visits between personal return objectives
- Flexible scheduling for individuals, friends, or small private groups

## Access Has Changed

Some former bases, compounds, military locations, or operational areas may have changed ownership, been demolished, become restricted, or no longer be accessible. We do not promise access to controlled locations. The final plan is based on what can be responsibly and practically arranged at the time of travel.

## Who This Journey Is For

Veterans and former personnel seeking a respectful, private return rather than a conventional group tour. Family members or companions can be incorporated into the plan when appropriate.

## Duration & Route

Duration is completely flexible. A journey may focus on one region or combine several parts of Afghanistan depending on the traveler's history, available time, and current route conditions.

## Start Planning Your Return

Tell us when you served, where you were based or traveled, the places you hope to revisit, and your preferred dates. We will use that information to develop a realistic private route.
