## Overview

Business & Investment is a private planning and ground-support service for companies, investors, delegations, and professionals traveling to Afghanistan for commercial objectives. It is not a fixed sightseeing itinerary: the program is built around the visitor's meetings, sectors, locations, schedule, and access requirements.

## What We Can Coordinate

- Airport arrival and departure support
- Hotels, vehicles, drivers, and intercity movement
- Meeting schedules and local appointment coordination
- Interpreters and practical local support when required
- Commercial, industrial, agricultural, and project site visits where access is available
- Route planning around the cities and provinces relevant to the assignment
- Cultural context and optional heritage visits around the working schedule

## Who This Service Is For

Corporate delegations, investors, consultants, project teams, technical specialists, researchers, and organizations that need a locally coordinated visit rather than a standard leisure tour.

## How Planning Works

Tell us the purpose of the visit, preferred dates, sectors of interest, people or organizations you expect to meet, and locations you need to reach. AfghanTours then develops a practical proposed program around travel time, local access, accommodation, transport, and current operating conditions.

## Access & Scheduling

Meetings and access to private, industrial, government, or project facilities depend on the relevant host or authority. Requested access should be treated as subject to confirmation rather than guaranteed.

## Accommodation & Transport

Hotels and transport are selected according to the working route, group size, schedule, and destination. Domestic flights or 4WD vehicles may be used where they are practical and available.

## Request a Proposal

There is no fixed duration or standard route. Send us your objectives and approximate dates and we will prepare a tailored plan.
