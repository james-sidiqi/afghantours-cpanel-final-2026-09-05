## Overview

Noshaq Expedition Support is designed for mountaineering teams attempting Afghanistan's highest peak. The program focuses on the logistics required to move a team from Kabul into Badakhshan, organize the approach, support the mountain phase, and return with enough flexibility for weather, access, and expedition timing.

## Expedition Support

- Kabul arrival, briefing, accommodation, and final preparation
- Badakhshan transport and approach logistics
- 4WD movement toward the expedition access route where practical
- Coordination of local transport, porter, animal, camp, or resupply requirements as applicable
- Base-camp and field-logistics planning
- Flexible mountain days for acclimatization, weather, and the team's climbing plan
- Extraction, return transport, and Kabul debrief

## Who This Is For

Experienced mountaineers and expedition teams arriving with a clear understanding of high-altitude climbing. The exact support package is scoped to the team's size, experience, equipment, objectives, and technical requirements.

## A Flexible 21-Day Framework

The 21-day program is an expedition framework rather than a rigid sightseeing itinerary. Mountain days must remain flexible. Weather, team condition, access, road conditions, permits, and climbing decisions can change the sequence.

## Accommodation & Transport

Kabul and Faizabad use the best suitable accommodation available for the expedition plan. The approach combines domestic air or road movement, 4WD transport, and field transport as required by the final route. Mountain accommodation is expedition camping.

## Important Expedition Note

Technical climbing decisions remain dependent on the expedition team's plan and the specialist mountain support confirmed for that departure. No summit outcome or fixed mountain schedule can be guaranteed.

## Best Time To Travel

The current operating window is June through August, subject to mountain conditions and the requirements of the individual expedition.
