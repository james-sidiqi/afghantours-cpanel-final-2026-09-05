Kandahar's pomegranates are among Afghanistan's most famous agricultural products. Their deep red color, sweetness, and export reputation make them a natural signature experience for the south.

## Orchard Country

During harvest season, orchards and markets fill with crates of fruit. Farmers sort, pack, sell, and share samples with the pride of people handling one of the province's best-known products.

## Fresh from the Source

Visitors can taste pomegranates in markets or, when access allows, near orchard areas. Fresh juice is the natural pairing: bright, sharp, sweet, and unmistakably Kandahari.

## Cultural Pairing: Fresh Pomegranate Juice

A glass of freshly pressed juice turns the fruit from a product into an experience. It connects agriculture, hospitality, trade, and place.
