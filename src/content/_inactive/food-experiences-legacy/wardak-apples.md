West of Kabul, Wardak is strongly associated with apples. During harvest season, roadsides and village markets fill with crates of fruit moving toward Kabul and beyond.

## Mountain Orchard Country

Cool nights, mountain-fed irrigation, and fertile valleys help produce apples that many Kabul families recognize and seek out.

## Harvest Season

From late summer into autumn, families pick, sort, sell, and transport apples. Travelers passing through can stop to taste and purchase directly from roadside vendors.

## Cultural Pairing: Fresh Apples and Green Tea

The best experience is simple: fresh apples, tea, and conversation during a central Afghanistan road journey.
