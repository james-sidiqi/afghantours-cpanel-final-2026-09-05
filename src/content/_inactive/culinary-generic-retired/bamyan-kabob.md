In Bamyan, kabob is highland hospitality: grilled meat, fresh bread, and a slower mountain pace after days among cliffs, lakes, and valleys.

## Kabob in the Highlands

Travelers typically encounter kabob as a shared meal rather than a destination restaurant brand. The pleasure is the setting as much as the plate — cool air, local bread, and conversation after sightseeing.

## Cultural Pairing: Bread and Highland Evenings

Kabob pairs with naan and tea, often as an evening meal after Buddha niches, city walks, or Band-e-Amir day trips.
