Kandahari rosh is slow-cooked meat — often lamb — associated with southern hospitality and shared meals. In Kandahar, the dish is less about a single named restaurant and more about the cooking tradition itself.

## A Southern Classic

Rosh is cooked low and slow until the meat softens and the broth deepens. It is typically eaten with fresh naan, simple accompaniments, and plenty of time at the table.

## Planning the Stop

Exact restaurant confirmation depends on the day’s routing and operator recommendations. The culinary experience is the dish and the hospitality around it, not a fixed public menu claim.

## Cultural Pairing: Naan and Tea

Rosh pairs naturally with hot naan and tea after a day in Kandahar’s historic landscape.
