### Treasures of the Silk Road

A deep dive into the historical heart of Afghanistan, crossing the Hindu Kush.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 10 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Year-round

### What This Tour Is For

This tour is designed for travelers who want a structured Afghanistan itinerary with local coordination, realistic routing, hotel planning, transport support, and guidance on cultural etiquette and access conditions.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
