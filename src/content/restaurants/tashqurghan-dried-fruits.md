Tashqurghan, also known as Khulm, has long been associated with trade routes, markets, dried fruits, and nuts. It is a natural pantry stop in northern Afghanistan.

## A Merchant Market

Products may include raisins, almonds, pistachios, walnuts, dried apricots, mulberries, figs, and sesame items. Vendors often invite tasting, comparison, and conversation before a purchase.

## The Silk Road Pantry

Dried fruits and nuts were ideal caravan foods: portable, nutritious, and durable. In Tashqurghan, that history still feels present in the market culture.

## Cultural Pairing: Green Tea and Mixed Dried Fruits

The simplest northern welcome is also one of the best: green tea with raisins, almonds, pistachios, and dried apricots.
