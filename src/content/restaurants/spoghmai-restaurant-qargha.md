Few places show the leisure side of Kabul better than Qargha Lake. Spoghmai Restaurant sits within that tradition of families, friends, and visitors leaving the city center for fresh air, water views, and a shared meal.

## Kabul's Escape to the Water

Qargha is where many Kabul residents go for weekend outings. The setting offers hills, water, open air, and a social atmosphere very different from the city streets.

## Lakeside Dining

Meals at Spoghmai are about scenery as much as food. Kabobs, rice, naan, salads, tea, and seasonal dishes are shared while guests look across the lake.

## Cultural Pairing: Qargha at Sunset

The ideal visit includes time along the water before or after the meal. As the light softens, Qargha becomes one of Kabul's most relaxed and photogenic social spaces.
