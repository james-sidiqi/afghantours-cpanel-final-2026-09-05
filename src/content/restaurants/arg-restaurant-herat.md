Arg Restaurant gives Herat a proper evening finale. After a day of citadels, mosques, bazaars, glass workshops, and tea houses, the table becomes the final cultural experience.

## The Afghan Feast

Guests can expect the kind of meal that makes Afghan hospitality memorable: rice, kabobs, naan, salads, yogurt sauces, tea, and dishes shared across the table.

## A Herat Evening

The best version of the experience begins in the Old City and ends at dinner. By then, visitors have seen Herat's architecture, crafts, and street life. The feast brings the day together through food and conversation.

## Cultural Pairing: Herat After Dark

Arg is strongest as part of an evening route: sunset in the city, dinner, tea, and a slower pace after a full day of exploration.
