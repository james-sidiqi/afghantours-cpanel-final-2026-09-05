On crisp windy afternoons, Afghan skies can fill with hundreds of kites. **Gudiparan Bazi** is not casual flying. It is strategy, patience, craft, and competition.

## The Art of Jang

The central contest is **jang**, or battle. Flyers maneuver their kites so the string crosses an opponent's line, then use coated string to cut it loose.

## Flyer and Spool Holder

A good team includes the flyer and the **charkha gir**, who manages the wooden spool, releasing and gathering line at exactly the right moment.

## The Azadi Kite

When a kite is cut free, it becomes an **azadi** kite. Children chase it through alleys and streets, hoping to claim the prize before it lands.

## Cultural Pairing: Saffron Tea and Nakhod

On a rooftop or high viewpoint, saffron tea, roasted chickpeas, raisins, and conversation make the sky-watching experience feel complete.
