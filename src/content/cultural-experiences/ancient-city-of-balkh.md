Balkh was once a great city of the north. Enough still stands to walk among the remains: the Bala Hisar and its walls, the ninth-century Haji Piyada mosque, the shrine of Abu Nasr Khwaja Parsa — also called the Green Mosque, or Rauza-e-Sharif — the tomb of the poet Rabia Balkhi, and the ruined house remembered as the madrasa of Rumi’s father, Bahauddin Walad.

We take these places together, as a single visit from Mazar-e-Sharif.
