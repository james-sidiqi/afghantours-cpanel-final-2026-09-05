Every Friday in different parts of Afghanistan, open spaces can transform into informal wrestling arenas. Spectators gather around a dirt ring as powerful competitors grip, lean, sweep, and throw in the tradition of **Pahlawani**.

## Strength and Honor

Pahlawani connects physical strength with humility, respect, and community reputation. Wrestlers begin and end with gestures of respect, and the winner often helps the defeated opponent back to his feet.

## The Atmosphere

The arena may be nothing more than a circle in the dirt. The front row of spectators becomes the boundary. Dust rises, commentary grows louder, and every successful throw brings a roar from the crowd.

## Cultural Pairing: Street-Side Bolani

A wrestling match pairs naturally with **Bolani** from nearby vendors: thin bread stuffed with potatoes, leeks, or herbs, fried on a hot griddle and served with yogurt sauce.

Schedules vary by neighborhood and season, so this experience must be arranged based on current local availability.
