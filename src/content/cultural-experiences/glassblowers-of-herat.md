Herat is one of Afghanistan's great craft cities. Its traditional glassblowers connect the city to Silk Road artistry, old bazaars, and the refined material culture of western Afghanistan.

## Fire, Breath, and Glass

Visitors may observe artisans working near furnaces, shaping molten glass into cups, vessels, ornaments, and decorative pieces. The process is physical, dangerous, and beautiful.

## A Herati Craft Identity

Glassblowing belongs naturally beside Herat's architecture, tilework, calligraphy, poetry, and saffron culture. It helps travelers understand the city as a place of makers, not only monuments.

## Cultural Pairing: Saffron Tea

The strongest pairing is Herati saffron tea served in handmade glassware. It combines two signatures of the province: craft and saffron.
