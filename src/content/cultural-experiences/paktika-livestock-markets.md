Paktika's strongest cultural story is pastoral rather than restaurant-based. Livestock, Kuchi movement, sheep, goats, and rural markets define much of the visitor experience.

## Market Day

Livestock markets are social and economic gatherings. Families and traders inspect animals, negotiate prices, exchange news, and prepare for ceremonies, feasts, or seasonal movement.

## Kuchi Connections

The province also connects naturally to nomadic and semi-nomadic traditions. Access depends heavily on route, timing, and local conditions.

## Cultural Pairing: Lamb Kabob and Fresh Naan

Market-day food should be direct and hearty: lamb, naan, tea, and simple hospitality.
