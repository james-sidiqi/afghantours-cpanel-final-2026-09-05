## Operator & Licensing

AfghanTours.com is owned and operated by **James Tourist & Travel Agency**, a licensed Afghan tour operator based in Kabul, Afghanistan.

### Company Details

- **Official Operator:** James Tourist & Travel Agency
- **License Number:** `ATO-KBL-1617`
- **Physical Office:**  
  2nd Floor, Majid Mall  
  Shahr-e-Naw, District 10  
  Kabul, Afghanistan

### Operating Presence

Planning and coordination are carried out from Kabul with Afghan guides, drivers, hotel partners, and regional contacts.

The company draws on more than 15 years of on-the-ground Afghanistan experience. This refers to the leadership team's experience in the country, not to 15 years of continuous AfghanTours operations.

### Travel Planning

Routes are planned around drive times, seasons, road conditions, access, and local coordination.

When circumstances change, the itinerary may change with them.

**Your safety is planned, not promised.**

### Letter of Invitation

Letter of Invitation support is provided for every booked tour.

Provincial travel permits for the booked itinerary are processed in advance.

AfghanTours does not issue visas.
