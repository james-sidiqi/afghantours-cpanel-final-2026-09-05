Badghis has one of Afghanistan's strongest agricultural identities: pistachios.

## Afghanistan's Green Gold

Many pistachios from Badghis are associated with naturally occurring pistachio forests across rolling hills. For local communities, harvest season has long meant food, trade, and income.

## A Taste of Badghis

Badghis pistachios are valued for flavor and regional identity. Markets may also sell raisins, almonds, and other dry goods, but pistachios remain the signature.

## Cultural Pairing: Green Tea and Pistachios

Tea with pistachios is a perfect Afghan welcome: simple, generous, and deeply tied to the landscape.
