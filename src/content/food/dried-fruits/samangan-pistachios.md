## Afghan Pistachios

Pistachios are part of Afghanistan’s dried-fruit and nut tradition, including harvests from the wild pistachio forests of Badghis and Samangan.

## The Pistachio Forests of Badghis

Badghis has a strong agricultural connection to pistachios. Naturally occurring trees grow across its rolling hills, and their seasonal harvest links local communities with markets and trade.

## Markets and Hospitality

Pistachios are shared with guests and served alongside green tea. Their flavor, availability, and presentation vary with the harvest and market. Forest visits depend on local access and the itinerary; pistachios can also be encountered in bazaars without visiting the forests.
