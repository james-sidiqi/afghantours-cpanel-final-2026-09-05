## Toot-e-Khoshk

Afghan mulberries are enjoyed fresh during their short harvest season and sun-dried to preserve them. Dried mulberries, known as toot-e-khoshk, are naturally sweet and form part of Afghanistan’s dried-fruit tradition.

## Walnuts, Tea, and Hospitality

Dried mulberries are often paired with walnuts, shared with guests, and carried as a simple travel snack. Look for them in dried-fruit shops and local bazaars, with quality and availability varying by harvest and storage.

## Fresh and Dried

[Fresh mulberries](/food-culture/produce/central-valley-mulberries/) belong to the spring and early summer harvest. Drying extends that harvest through the year and gives the fruit a different texture and flavor.
