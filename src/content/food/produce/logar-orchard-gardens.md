Logar's orchard country is known for gardens and fruit-growing areas that help supply nearby Kabul markets, giving travelers a broader view of the province's agricultural identity.

## Kabul's Orchard Province

Logar produces apples, apricots, cherries, pears, walnuts, and other seasonal produce. Its green valleys contrast sharply with the dry mountain edges around them.

## Roadside Fruit Culture

Travelers may encounter fruit stands, village markets, and farms that show the relationship between Kabul and its surrounding agricultural provinces.

## Cultural Pairing: Fresh Fruit and Green Tea

A Logar stop is best kept simple: seasonal fruit, tea, local bread, and a glimpse of rural life close to the capital.
