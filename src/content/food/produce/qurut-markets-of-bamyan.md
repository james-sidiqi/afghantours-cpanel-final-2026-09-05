On the road from Bamyan City toward Band-e-Amir, small dairy markets offer one of Afghanistan's most powerful tastes: **qurut**, dried salty yogurt.

## A Highland Food

Qurut is made by salting strained yogurt, shaping it into small balls, and drying it until hard. It preserves dairy nutrition for long periods and has supported mountain communities through harsh seasons.

## The Brave Traveler's Challenge

Guides often offer visitors a small piece with a smile. The taste is intensely salty and tangy. Reactions are part of the fun.

## Roadside Dairy Culture

The markets may also sell fresh yogurt, butter, cream, cheese, and other local dairy products. They offer a quick but memorable look at Bamyan's rural food economy.

## Cultural Pairing: Band-e-Amir and the Central Highlands

Qurut belongs to the road, the pastures, and the highland journey. Tasting it before reaching Band-e-Amir makes the landscape feel more connected to the people who live there.
