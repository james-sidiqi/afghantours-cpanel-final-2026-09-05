### Wardak Apples

Wardak apples are one of the province's best-known seasonal products. Orchards in the high valleys produce fruit that appears in local markets during the late-summer and autumn harvest period.

### Season

Our current produce catalog lists the main season from **August through October**. Exact harvest timing varies from year to year.

### Food & Culture Context

These are not a staged food experience. Travelers may encounter Wardak apples in markets, roadside produce stops, homes, or meals when the season and route line up.

Their value on a journey is simple: they connect the highland road with the agricultural communities along it.

### Visiting Wardak

Wardak is commonly crossed on central-highland routes, but stops depend on the day's road plan, timing, and local conditions.
