### Overview
Spinach and herbs sautéed or slowly cooked with onions and Afghan spices.

### Dietary Notes
Vegetarian; vegan when prepared without ghee, dairy or meat stock.

Vegan travelers should inform AfghanTours in advance. We will confirm with the establishment whether vegetable oil is used instead of ghee or other animal fats, and check for dairy, meat stock and other animal-derived ingredients. Vegan preparation cannot be guaranteed without confirmation. Shared cooking surfaces may also affect suitability; please tell us if you need to avoid cross-contact.

### Where to Try It
This dish may be available in Afghan homes, restaurants, or markets depending on region, season and local preparation. We do not represent it as exclusive to a particular province.

### Traveler Notes
Availability and ingredients vary. Ask the AfghanTours team to include suitable food stops in your itinerary.
