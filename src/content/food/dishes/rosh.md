## Rosh Across Afghanistan

Rosh, also written Rosht, is a slow-cooked meat dish often made with lamb or mutton. It is traditionally shared with warm Afghan naan and simple accompaniments. Recipes and serving styles vary between households, cooks, and places.

## At the Table

Gentle cooking brings the meat to a tender texture. Potatoes and other vegetables may accompany it, as shown in these photos. Portions and presentation range from individual plates to larger dishes shared around the table.
