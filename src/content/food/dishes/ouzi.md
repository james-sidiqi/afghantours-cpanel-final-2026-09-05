### Overview
A celebratory preparation of whole sheep or lamb stuffed with seasoned rice and slowly cooked for sharing.

### Dietary Notes
Contains meat.

For vegan or vegetarian travelers, AfghanTours must confirm ingredients and preparation directly with the cook or restaurant. Shared cooking surfaces, dairy toppings, animal fats and meat stock may affect suitability.

### Where to Try It
This dish may be available in Afghan homes, restaurants, or markets depending on region, season and local preparation. We do not represent it as exclusive to a particular province.
