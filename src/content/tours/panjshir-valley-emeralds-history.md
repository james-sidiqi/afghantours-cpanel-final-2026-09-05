### Panjshir Valley: Emeralds & History

A short but powerful trip north of Kabul into the stunning Panjshir Valley.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 4 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Intermediate
- Season: Apr–Oct

### What This Tour Is For

This short journey is for travelers who want one focused day in Panjshir without building an unrealistic multi-day valley itinerary around it. The route runs north through Parwan, with village, river, modern-history, and emerald-working context included when access allows.
