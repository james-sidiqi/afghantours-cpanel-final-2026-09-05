### Panjshir Valley: Emeralds & History

A short but powerful trip north of Kabul into the stunning Panjshir Valley.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 4 days / 3 nights
- Tour type: private, or a scheduled shared departure
- Group: private from 1 traveler; shared departures need a minimum of 2
- Transport: private 4WD
- Accommodation: Kabul for all three nights; no overnight in Panjshir
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Intermediate
- Season: Apr–Oct

### What This Tour Is For

This short journey is for travelers who want one focused day in Panjshir without building an unrealistic multi-day valley itinerary around it. The route runs north through Parwan and Jabal Saraj, with the Panjshir River, mountain villages, modern history and the emerald trade included when access allows, and returns to Kabul the same day.

### The emerald experience

- An introduction to the local emerald industry is part of the Panjshir day.
- Attending an emerald auction or an arranged gemstone trading session is optional and available only by special arrangement on privately coordinated tours.
- Attendance, successful bidding and purchases cannot be promised.
- Gemstone purchases cannot automatically be exported. Legitimate auction access, seller provenance, mineral trading rules, taxes, customs documentation and export permissions are verified before the activity is confirmed.
- An ordinary gemstone shop visit is not presented as an auction.
- If a prearranged auction and the Paryan Valley extension cannot both fit, the auction takes priority.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, permits, and current local operating requirements.
