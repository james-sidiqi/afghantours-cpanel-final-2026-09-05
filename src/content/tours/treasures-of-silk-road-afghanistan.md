### Treasures of the Silk Road

A deep dive into the historical heart of Afghanistan, crossing the Hindu Kush.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 10 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Year-round

### What This Tour Is For

This ten-day journey connects Kabul, Bamyan, Herat, Balkh, and Samangan through a sequence of major historical landscapes rather than a single continuous road route. It is designed for travelers who want Buddhist, Timurid, Islamic, and Silk Road heritage in one itinerary while accepting the practical mix of flights and overland travel.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
