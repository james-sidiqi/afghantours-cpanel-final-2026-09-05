### Overview
Short on time? Get a curated, authentic three-day snapshot (3 days / 2 nights) of Kabul’s architecture, culture, food, and history.

---

### Why This Trip Matters
A compact weekend itinerary featuring Darulaman Palace, Bagh-e Babur, Shah-e-Du Shamshira, Karte Sakhi Shrine, plus local crafts and food stops—carefully curated for a safe, authentic experience.
