### Overview

Seven days from Kabul into Bamyan and back. The road day is long. The valley days are the reason for the road.

Kabul frames the journey: the National Museum, Darulaman Palace and Babur’s Gardens on arrival, and on the last full day the Mosque of the Two Swords, Kahfroshi Bird Market and the view from Wazir Akbar Khan Hill. Chicken Street is kept for the final morning.

In Bamyan there are three nights: the Buddha Niches, Shahr-e-Zohak and Shahr-e-Gholghola, the market and its qurut, and a full day for Band-e-Amir when the weather, the road and park access agree.

### Accommodation

- Kabul: nights 1, 5 and 6, Park Star Hotel (standard)
- Bamyan: nights 2, 3 and 4, Gholghola Hotel (standard)

Final hotels, timing, and price are confirmed after we know group size, dates, and current access.
