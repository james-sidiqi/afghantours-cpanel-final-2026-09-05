### Kabul & Surroundings

This tour focuses on Kabul and its surrounding regions, offering visits to historical sites, gardens, and museums.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 6 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Leisurely
- Season: Year-round

### What This Tour Is For

This trip uses Kabul as the base for a deeper look at the capital and selected day routes beyond it. The current itinerary combines Kabul heritage with a Panjshir day, a conditional eastern route toward Nangarhar, and a western Kabul/Paghman day, with outside excursions adjusted when access or timing does not support them.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
