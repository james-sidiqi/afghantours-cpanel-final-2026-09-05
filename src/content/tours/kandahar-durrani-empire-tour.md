### Kandahar: The Durrani Empire

Kandahar is the historical and cultural soul of the Pashtun people.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 6 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Year-round

### What This Tour Is For

This journey is for travelers interested in the political and cultural history of Kandahar, particularly the Durrani and Hotak periods. The current itinerary includes dedicated time in Kandahar for historic shrines, monuments, city context, and Chilzina when timing and access permit.
