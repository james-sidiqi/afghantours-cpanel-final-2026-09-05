### Afghanistan Discovery Tour Spring

The Discovery Tour is a balanced overview of Afghanistan’s highlights, combining major cities, cultural landmarks, and natural scenery.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 10 days / 9 nights
- Route: Kabul → Herat → Kabul → Bamyan → Band-e-Amir → Kabul
- Transport: domestic flights and private 4WD
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Group departures: minimum 6 confirmed participants
- Season: Apr-Jun

### What This Tour Is For

This ten-day journey is for travelers who want a broad introduction to Afghanistan through Kabul, Herat, and Bamyan in spring. It combines major heritage sites with overland movement into the central highlands while keeping the Bamyan road and Band-e-Amir day responsive to actual conditions.

### Where You Stay

- Kabul: nights 1, 2, 5 and 9
- Herat: nights 3 and 4
- Bamyan: nights 6, 7 and 8

### Conditional activities

- The Kabul–Herat and Herat–Kabul flights depend on airline schedules; Chicken Street on Day 5 is added only if the flight arrives in time.
- Band-e-Amir access depends on the season, the road and visitor restrictions.
- Day 9 is a road-transfer day. The OMAR Landmine Museum, the Mir Zaman box-camera portrait and the optional community-support visit happen only if the return journey leaves time, and only by prior arrangement.
- Community experiences run only with a consenting host organization, with participant privacy and safeguarding respected.
- Whether the Afghan clothing on Day 1 is included in the price is confirmed on your written proposal.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
