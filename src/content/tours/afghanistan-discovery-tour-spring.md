### Afghanistan Discovery Tour Spring

The Discovery Tour is a balanced overview of Afghanistan’s highlights, combining major cities, cultural landmarks, and natural scenery.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 9 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Apr-Jun

### What This Tour Is For

This nine-day journey is for travelers who want a broad introduction to Afghanistan through Kabul, Herat, and Bamyan during the April–June travel window. It combines major heritage sites with overland movement into the central highlands while keeping the Bamyan road and Band-e-Amir day responsive to actual conditions.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
