### Winter Circuit

A 12-day seasonal journey linking Kabul, the northern plains, and the central highlands while keeping enough flexibility for winter roads, weather, and domestic flight schedules.

### The Route

The journey begins with time in Kabul before moving north toward Mazar-e-Sharif and Ancient Balkh. Depending on the operating plan, northern travel may be by road or domestic flight.

A route-dependent day can use Samangan's Buddhist heritage sites or the Kunduz agricultural belt before the itinerary returns toward Kabul.

The second half of the journey is planned around Bamyan when winter road access permits. If the road does not support the planned movement, the itinerary is adjusted rather than forcing the route.

### Winter in Bamyan

When access allows, the Bamyan portion combines valley heritage sites with winter landscapes and a weather-dependent day toward Band-e-Amir or viewpoints in the Koh-e-Baba area.

Snow, road conditions, daylight, and temperature all affect what is practical on a given departure.

### What This Tour Is For

This journey is for travelers who want to see Afghanistan outside the main spring and autumn travel seasons and who are comfortable with an itinerary that remains responsive to winter conditions.

The northern route, the choice between road and flight, and access to Bamyan can all change as conditions change.

### Planning Notes

Winter travel requires additional schedule flexibility. Road movement, domestic flights, overnight points, and activity sequencing are reviewed against current weather, road access, daylight, and local operating conditions before and during the journey.

Letter of Invitation support is included for booked travel. We do not issue visas.
