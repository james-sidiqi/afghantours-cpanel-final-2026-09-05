### Afghanistan Discovery Tour Fall

The Discovery Tour is a balanced overview of Afghanistan’s highlights, combining major cities, cultural landmarks, and natural scenery.

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 9 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Sep-Nov

### What This Tour Is For

This nine-day journey follows a Kabul–Herat–Bamyan arc during the September–November travel window. It is designed for travelers who want several of Afghanistan’s major cultural regions in one trip without pretending that flights, highland roads, or Band-e-Amir access will always run exactly to schedule.

### Planning Notes

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
