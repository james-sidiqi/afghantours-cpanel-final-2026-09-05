### The Timurid Grandeur of Herat

A focused exploration of Herat, often called the "Pearl of Khorasan".

### Tour Profile

- Duration: 7 days
- Travel style: Cultural / Scenic
- Activity level: Moderate
- Season: Year-round

### What This Tour Is For

This seven-day journey is for travelers whose main interest is Herat’s Timurid and Islamic heritage. After initial context in Kabul, the itinerary concentrates on Herat’s Friday Mosque, citadel, minarets, tilework, old-city setting, and a flexible cultural day before returning to Kabul.

Final routing, hotels, transport, and pricing should be confirmed after reviewing group size, travel dates, road conditions, flight availability, permits, and current local operating requirements.
