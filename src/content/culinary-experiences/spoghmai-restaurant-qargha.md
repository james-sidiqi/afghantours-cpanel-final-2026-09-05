Spoghmai Restaurant sits on the shores of Qargha Lake, a favorite Kabul weekend escape. Meals here are as much about the setting — pines, outdoor seating, and the lake — as about the food.

## Lakeside Setting

Qargha draws Kabul residents for outings, walks, and family meals. Spoghmai's terraces and garden seating look out over the water toward the surrounding hills.

## Pairing with a Day Trip

A stop at Spoghmai pairs naturally with Paghman, Istalif pottery visits, or a longer circuit around western Kabul.
