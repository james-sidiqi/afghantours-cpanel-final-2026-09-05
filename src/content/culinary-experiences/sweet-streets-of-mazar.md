Mazar-e-Sharif is not only the Blue Mosque and buzkashi. It is also a city of sweets, tea houses, nuts, sesame, and northern Afghan food traditions. This culinary experience explores Mazar’s traditional sweets and market culture rather than a single dessert.

## Sweet Shops and Bazaars

Visitors may find sesame sweets, halwa, noql, roasted nuts, dried fruit, and traditional candies sold near markets and gathering places.

## Sesame Sweets, Halwa and Regional Pastries

Sesame delicacies, halwa and regional pastries are part of the northern sweets tradition. Northern Afghanistan also has rice traditions that differ from Kabul: sesame seed palaw adds aroma, nuttiness, and texture, reflecting the agriculture and tastes of the region.

## Tea Houses and Northern Hospitality

A walk near the Blue Mosque followed by tea, sweets, and market browsing gives visitors a grounded sense of everyday Mazar life.

## Planning Notes

Shops, stalls and what is on sale vary by season and day. We plan this stop around your Mazar-e-Sharif route and confirm timing locally. The photograph shows Mazar-e-Sharif for regional context.
