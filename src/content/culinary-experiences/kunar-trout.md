Kunar's mountain rivers, forested valleys, and roadside cooking traditions make freshly grilled trout one of the province's most distinctive food experiences. From the Jalalabad hub, this is typically planned as a day-trip food stop rather than a fixed city restaurant.

## River Valley Food

At riverside restaurants and roadside grills, fresh fish can be prepared simply with salt, spices, heat, and bread. The setting does much of the work: moving water, mountain air, and green valley scenery.

## The Experience

Travelers stop near the river, watch the food being prepared, and eat with tandoor naan, tea, and local conversation. Exact grill locations vary by season and route conditions; your guide confirms the stop on the day.

## Cultural Pairing: Grilled Trout and Tandoor Naan

This is one of the most direct pairings on the site: fish from the river, bread from the tandoor, and the landscape around both.
