This experience showcases Herat’s saffron heritage and the opportunity to sample locally prepared saffron ice cream. It is a culinary experience in the Herat hub, not one of our core dessert entries.

## Herat’s Saffron Heritage

Afghan saffron is grown mostly in Herat Province, which produces most of the country’s saffron. Saffron connects Herat’s fields, bazaars and kitchens.

## Saffron in Regional Sweets

Afghan saffron flavors regional sweets and drinks, from saffron tea to ice cream flavored with Afghan saffron and regional ingredients.

## Tasting Saffron Ice Cream

Where it is available, travelers can try locally prepared saffron ice cream as part of a Herat food and bazaar day. Actual tasting arrangements remain subject to local availability and season; we do not name a specific vendor in advance.

## Herat Culinary Travel

Pair this stop with saffron tea houses, the old city and the citadel when planning your days in Herat.
