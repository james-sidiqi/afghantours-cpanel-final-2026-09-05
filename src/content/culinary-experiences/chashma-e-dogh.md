Chashma-e-Dogh is one of Kabul's most recognizable food-and-social gathering places. The name points to dogh, the chilled yogurt drink that gives the area its identity.

## More Than a Drink

Dogh may sound simple: yogurt, water, salt, mint, and local herbs. But in Kabul's warm months, few drinks are more refreshing or more closely tied to family outings.

## A Social Space

Families gather, pilgrims pass through, children play, vendors call out, and groups sit together over food and drinks. The experience offers a direct look at ordinary Kabul life beyond formal monuments.

## Cultural Pairing: Istalif and Western Kabul

Chashma-e-Dogh pairs naturally with Istalif, Qargha, Paghman, and Sakhi-area visits. It gives travelers a relaxed local stop after craft workshops, shrines, or city views.
