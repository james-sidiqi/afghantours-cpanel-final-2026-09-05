Palaw — Afghanistan’s celebrated rice hospitality — belongs in Ghazni as much as in any historic stop along the southern and central corridors. This culinary experience focuses on shared rice meals rather than a single named restaurant.

## Rice as Hospitality

A strong palaw platter is an act of welcome: rice, meat, and the time taken to cook and share it. In Ghazni, the dish sits naturally after citadel views, minarets, and city walks.

## Cultural Pairing: Kabuli Pulao Tradition

Travelers who already know [Kabuli pulao](/food-culture/kabuli-pulao/) will recognize the broader rice tradition; Ghazni palaw is the local stop for that hospitality on a hub day.
