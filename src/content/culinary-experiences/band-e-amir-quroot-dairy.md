On the road from Bamyan City toward Band-e-Amir, small dairy markets offer one of Afghanistan's most powerful tastes: **quroot** (also spelled qurut), dried salty yogurt.

## A Highland Food

Quroot is made by salting strained yogurt, shaping it into small balls, and drying it until hard. It preserves dairy nutrition for long periods and has supported mountain communities through harsh seasons.

## The Brave Traveler's Challenge

Guides often offer visitors a small piece with a smile. The taste is intensely salty and tangy. Reactions are part of the fun.

## Roadside Dairy Culture

The markets may also sell fresh yogurt, butter, cream, cheese, and other local dairy products. They offer a quick but memorable look at Bamyan's rural food economy.
