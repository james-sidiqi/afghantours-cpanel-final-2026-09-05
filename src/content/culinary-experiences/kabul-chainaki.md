Chainaki is one of Kabul’s most distinctive comfort dishes: meat and broth slow-cooked in a metal teapot (*chainak*), then poured over bread. It is everyday food with a strong local identity.

## The Teapot Stew

The method is simple and memorable. Meat, fat, and aromatics cook gently in the teapot until the broth is rich. Bread softens under the pour, turning the meal into a shared, hands-on experience.

## Where Travelers Encounter It

This culinary experience focuses on the dish and dining custom rather than a single named restaurant. Exact venues are confirmed by operators based on neighborhood, timing, and traveler preferences.

## Cultural Pairing: Bread and Green Tea

Chainaki belongs with fresh bread and tea — a Kabul lunch or early dinner that feels local without needing ceremony.
