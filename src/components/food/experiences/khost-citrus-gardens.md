Khost surprises many visitors. Its warmer climate supports citrus, creating a food identity very different from Afghanistan's highland provinces.

## Citrus Country

Oranges, lemons, and other citrus fruits appear in markets and orchards, giving Khost a bright agricultural signature.

## Fresh Juice

Fresh orange juice is the natural pairing: simple, refreshing, and tied directly to the region's climate.

## Cultural Pairing: Citrus and Market Hospitality

A Khost market stop can introduce visitors to a side of Afghanistan they rarely expect: green valleys, fruit stalls, and subtropical produce.
