Kunar should be fish. Its rivers, forests, and valleys make grilled river trout one of the province's clearest food identities.

## River Valley Food

At riverside restaurants and roadside grills, fresh fish can be prepared simply with salt, spices, heat, and bread. The setting does much of the work: moving water, mountain air, and green valley scenery.

## The Experience

Travelers stop near the river, watch the food being prepared, and eat with tandoor naan, tea, and local conversation.

## Cultural Pairing: Grilled Trout and Tandoor Naan

This is one of the most direct pairings on the site: fish from the river, bread from the tandoor, and the landscape around both.
