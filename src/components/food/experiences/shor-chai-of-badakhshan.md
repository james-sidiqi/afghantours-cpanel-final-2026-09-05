In Badakhshan, warmth matters. **Shor chai** is a savory tea made with salt, milk, and sometimes butter or cream, designed for mountain climates and long conversations.

## Not Sweet Tea

Visitors expecting ordinary tea are often surprised. Shor chai is salty, rich, and practical. It provides warmth, calories, and comfort in cold regions.

## Hospitality in the Highlands

In homes, tea houses, and roadside stops, guests are offered tea before anything else. Shor chai may be served with bread, dried mulberries, walnuts, yogurt, or local snacks.

## Cultural Pairing: Faizabad Bazaar

The best introduction is a tea stop in or around Faizabad, where traders, travelers, and local families gather before journeys deeper into Badakhshan or toward Wakhan.
