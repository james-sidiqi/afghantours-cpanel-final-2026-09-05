Farah gives AfghanTours a very different food geography: dates, palms, heat, and desert agriculture.

## Oasis Agriculture

Date palms show how communities cultivate food in dry western landscapes. The groves and markets reveal a side of Afghanistan many visitors do not expect.

## Fresh and Dried Dates

Depending on season, travelers may encounter fresh dates, dried dates, or market stalls selling products from nearby groves.

## Cultural Pairing: Dates and Green Tea

Dates with green tea are simple, durable, and connected to hospitality across dry regions. In Farah, the pairing fits the landscape perfectly.
