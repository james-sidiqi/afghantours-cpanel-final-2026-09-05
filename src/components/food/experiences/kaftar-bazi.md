As the late afternoon light softens over Kabul, attention often shifts upward. On rooftops across the city, pigeon keepers release their flocks into the sky and guide them with whistles, cloths, poles, and practiced instinct.

## More Than a Hobby

For a dedicated **kaftar baz**, pigeons are a lifelong passion. Birds are bred, trained, recognized, and valued according to flight behavior, stamina, color, and lineage.

## The Sky as Arena

The play can become a tactical contest between neighboring rooftops. Keepers try to guide their flock, confuse rival birds, and sometimes lure an opponent's pigeon back to their own loft.

## Rooftop Social Life

Kaftar bazi also creates a quiet refuge above the noise of the city. Friends gather, drink tea, discuss birds, and watch the sky change.

## Cultural Pairing: Cardamom Tea and Noql

The best introduction is a private rooftop visit with tea, noql, and the sound of wings overhead. This is not a mass-tour experience; it depends on trusted access and respectful hosting.
