Ghazni is remembered for empire, scholarship, poetry, and old roads. It is also known by many travelers for sweets carried home from the journey.

## The Sweet Stop

**Sheer pira** is a milk-based sweet often served with tea or purchased as a gift. For travelers moving between Kabul and the south, a sweets stop in Ghazni adds a living food tradition to the historic route.

## Tea House Culture

After visiting the minarets, citadel area, or Sultan Mahmud's tomb, tea and sweets give visitors a slower, local moment.

## Cultural Pairing: Green Tea

The richness of sheer pira works best with green tea. Together they create a simple but memorable Ghazni experience.
