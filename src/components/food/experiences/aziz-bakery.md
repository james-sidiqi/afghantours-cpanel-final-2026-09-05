Chicken Street is known for carpets, antiques, jewelry, gifts, and handicrafts. Aziz Bakery adds another layer to the experience: Kabul sweets.

## A Kabul Sweet Stop

For family visits, birthdays, Eid, engagements, and everyday treats, bakeries play an important role in Kabul social life. Display cases filled with cakes, cookies, pastries, and crème rolls show how Afghan celebration culture has absorbed influences from the region and beyond.

## The Famous Crème Rolls

Crisp pastry shells filled with sweet cream are among the bakery treats many visitors remember. A small box can become a gift, a snack, or a reason to sit down for tea.

## Cultural Pairing: Chicken Street and Afternoon Tea

The best experience is to combine a walk through Chicken Street with sweets and tea. It is part shopping excursion, part cultural immersion, and part taste of Kabul family life.
