Mazar-e-Sharif is not only the Blue Mosque and buzkashi. It is also a city of sweets, tea houses, nuts, sesame, and northern Afghan food traditions.

## Sweets Around the Bazaar

Visitors may find sesame sweets, halwa, noql, roasted nuts, dried fruit, and traditional candies sold near markets and gathering places.

## Sesame Seed Palaw

Northern Afghanistan has rice traditions that differ from Kabul. Sesame seed palaw adds aroma, nuttiness, and texture, reflecting the agriculture and tastes of the region.

## Cultural Pairing: Blue Mosque Tea Culture

A walk near the Blue Mosque followed by tea, sweets, and market browsing gives visitors a grounded sense of everyday Mazar life.
