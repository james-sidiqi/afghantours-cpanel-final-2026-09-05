Herat is Afghanistan's saffron capital, and tea is one of the simplest ways visitors can taste that identity.

## The Herati Tea House

After visiting the citadel, Friday Mosque, and bazaars, a tea house stop lets the pace slow down. Saffron tea arrives fragrant and golden, often accompanied by sweets such as gosh-e-fil.

## Saffron and City Identity

Saffron connects Herat to farming communities outside the city, export markets, and a refined culinary tradition. It is one of the province's most recognizable products.

## Cultural Pairing: Gosh-e-Fil

The crisp sweetness of gosh-e-fil balances the floral warmth of saffron tea. Together they make a distinctly Herati pause in the middle of a historic city.
